# Screen — May 2026 final cut

Stricter application of the framework than prior `final.md`. To enter
Tier 1 a name must satisfy:

- **Triangulation 3/3** (PF/through-cycle + cap-stack game theory +
  insider/insti revealed preference) — OR founder-on-both-sides-of-cap-
  stack as a substitute
- **Alignment-gap ≥ 2×** OR **sovereign industrial-policy anchor (A2)**
  OR **strategic premium-to-VWAP placement**
- **Condition 7 catalyst dated within 24 months** (not just "plausible")
- **Bucket A or B instrument accessible** (not C-only)
- **Zero active red flags** (court-reversal risk, insider net seller,
  state-backstop conditional, second restructuring filed)

Under this discipline the top tier compresses meaningfully.

### What's not in this screen — the discipline cost

By requiring C7 dated within 24 months I drop:
- Pure binaries with no catalyst date (Chinese property → Tier 2)
- Microcap stubs where operational delivery is unmeasured (French stubs
  → Tier 2)
- Watch-list pre-trigger names (Thyssenkrupp, Kenya Airways → Watch)

By requiring zero active red flags I drop:
- Vanke (state backstop conditional)
- Novaland (founder net seller)
- Casino (March 2026 balance-sheet adapting announcement)

By requiring Bucket A/B accessible I drop:
- All US Ch.11 emergences that went private
- Most Indian IBC outcomes
- Steinhoff residual

The strictness shrinks the top tier from 25 (in `final.md`) to 10
(here), but the 10 are the names where *the framework's questions all
answer "yes" simultaneously with dated catalysts*. That's the
Tenneco-grade threshold.
