WHAT QUALIFIES AS ASYMMETRIC HERE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every setup below clears all four screens:

  1. UPSIDE: plausible 2-5x in 12-36 months (or 10x+ for the warrant cases)
  2. DOWNSIDE: defined floor - control stake, sponsor cost basis, cash/NAV,
     tender price, royalty stream, or producing asset value
  3. CATALYST: dated, public, and observable - not "value will be recognized"
  4. SIGNAL: a SITG fund has put real % of their book or own money into it,
     concentrated enough that they cannot exit quietly

Setups are ranked by RISK-ADJUSTED MULTIPLE (expected upside / probable
downside), not by absolute upside.

                                 MNKTQ/VRM post-BK equity tails.
