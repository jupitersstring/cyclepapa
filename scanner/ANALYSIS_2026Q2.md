# Godley SFC Bull-Market Scanner — June 2026 Scan

*Generated from the scanner's June 2026 calibrated dataset. Every figure below
is the scanner's own output, not commentary. Methodology: `scanner/README.md`.*

The scanner reads asset markets through Wynne Godley's lens — **asset prices
rise where net new financing into private balance sheets is expanding and
accelerating, and where that fuel translates into actual corporate profits**
(the Kalecki-Levy bridge), discounted by valuation, crowding, data quality,
and stock-flow sustainability.

- **Bottom-right (low fuel, expensive q) = the trap:** India (q 1.31, fuel

  fading.

## 9. One-paragraph house view

**The cleanest bull setups are the two fiscal-pivot mercantilist savers
(Germany, Korea) and the commodity-rent complex (Saudi/UAE/Qatar/Chile),
plus Brazil at the top on cheap-valuation-plus-carry.** The clearest avoids
are the Anglo-mimic deficit economies (UK worst, then Australia/NZ/Canada)
where the SFC identity is forcing households back into surplus while fiscal
tightens — Godley's textbook recession configuration — and the expensive,
profit-fading US/India. Japan has the best raw profit decomposition on the
board but is held back by valuation (q 1.21) and crowding; it is a "right
thesis, late entry" rather than a fresh opportunity. The one genuine
Godley-projection warning is **Romania** (NIIP −33pp over 5y); the credit-fuelled
bulls (Brazil, Poland, Turkey) are real but carry the Seven-Processes flags
that say the fuel itself is the fragility.
