Opp ±σ shows the score with a ±1σ error bar (uncertainty sized by data confidence). When the bar straddles the centre line the sign is not distinguishable from neutral — marked with a ? and a faint row. Hover for confidence and the top drivers. N / L places each market's edge on the horizon: the circle is the NEAR (liquidity, <1y) signal, the square the LONG (fundamental, 3–4y) signal, lapis when favourable; ‡ marks credit-fuelled setups that the backtest says reverse at 2–3 years. Balances →0 is the Godley identity made visible: three ink bars — Private, Government, eXternal — diverge left (borrowing) and right (saving) around a centre axis and always sum to zero. Cycle is a tick on a 0–100 debt-clock line, coloured by regime; faint ticks mark the 30/55/75 turns. Factor band, numbered 1–6 left→right: (1) profit fuel (2) credit impulse (3) valuation, cheap (4) carry (5) un-crowding (6) robustness — bars rise lapis when favourable, fall crimson when adverse, height is the cross-sectional z-score; hover any bar for its value. A faint wash marks the top and bottom opportunity deciles; fragility turns crimson past 0.50; † flags low data confidence. A reading of configuration on calibrated mid-2026 data, not a timed forecast — after Wynne Godley, Levy Economics Institute of Bard College.
