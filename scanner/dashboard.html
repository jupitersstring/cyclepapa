The Godley Lattice

Reading the world economy the way Wynne Godley did — as sectoral balance sheets. Every economy is a configuration of three balances that sum to zero; the question is whether purchasing power is flowing into private balance sheets, and whether the resulting debts can be sustained.

Reading
① Identity
The three balances — Private, Government, eXternal — sum to zero. A bar left of centre drains demand; right injects it.
② Flows
Is purchasing power reaching profits? The Kalecki-Levy fuel: investment + govt deficit + net exports − household saving.
③ Stocks
Do the implied debt / external paths hold, or explode? Godley's sustainability test.
④ Fragility
Is financing hedge (income), speculative (rollover), or Ponzi (asset-price)? — Minsky.
⑤ Verdict
Given the four: bull or bear, and where in the debt cycle (0–100).
