DISCLOSURE TYPES BY JURISDICTION
================================================================================

UNITED STATES — SEC EDGAR
--------------------------
Form 13F-HR     Quarterly holdings >$100M AUM threshold
                Filed 45 days after quarter-end
                Reports: ticker, shares, value, put/call, voting authority
                Limitations: 45-day stale, omits shorts, options, sovereign,
                  private, foreign, and excluded confidential treatment

Form 13F-NT     Combined report when filing covers omitted holdings

Schedule 13D    >5% beneficial ownership w/ INTENT to influence
                Filed within 10 days of crossing threshold
                Item 4 = purpose (KEY — read for activist intent)
                Item 5 = beneficial ownership detail
                Item 6 = contracts/arrangements
                Item 7 = exhibits (letters, MoU, agreements)
                Amendments (13D/A) on any material change

Schedule 13G    >5% beneficial ownership w/ PASSIVE intent
                Filed within 45 days of year-end (eligible investors)
                Less actionable than 13D but still useful

Form 4          Insider transactions (officers, directors, 10%+)
                Filed within 2 business days
                Reports: purchase, sale, gift, option exercise
                CRITICAL: insider buying at depressed levels = Yartseva signal

Form 3          Initial insider statement of holdings

Form 5          Annual statement of changes for insiders

Form NPX        Voting records (annual, by Aug 31)

Form ADV        Investment adviser registration (Parts 1, 2A, 2B)
                Filed annually + amendments
                Reports: AUM, strategies, custodian, fees, conflicts

Form PF         Private fund reporting (confidential to SEC, occasionally
                surfaces in litigation)

Form D          Reg D offerings (private placements; can reveal new funds)

SC TO-T         Tender offer schedule (acquirer)

SC TO-I         Issuer self-tender / buyback

S-4 / S-1       Registration statements (M&A, IPO)

DEF 14A         Proxy statements (incl. activist proposals)

DEFC 14A        Contested proxy materials

Item 5.07       8-K item disclosing voting results

10-K / 10-Q     Annual / quarterly reports (insider holdings tables,
                  related party transactions, segment data)


UNITED KINGDOM — FCA + Companies House
---------------------------------------
TR-1            Major shareholding notification
                Threshold: 3%, then each 1% point thereafter
                Filed within 2 trading days of crossing
                Reports: party, instrument, voting rights
                Indirect holdings via swaps/CFDs disclosed separately

Schedule 1 Form Substantial shareholding (UK Listed - subject to DTR 5)

DTR 5 disclosures Continuing disclosure regime

PSC (Companies House) Persons with Significant Control (>25% / control)


EUROPEAN UNION — National regulators
------------------------------------
France (AMF):       Declaration de franchissement de seuil
                    Thresholds: 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 1/3, 50%, 2/3, 90%, 95%
                    Plus 90% mandatory bid threshold

Germany (BaFin):    Stimmrechtsmitteilung (WpHG §33-34)
                    Thresholds: 3%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 50%, 75%
                    Plus financial instruments disclosure

Italy (CONSOB):     Communicazioni delle partecipazioni rilevanti
                    Thresholds: 3% (small caps 5%), 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%,
                                30%, 50%, 66.6%, 90%

Spain (CNMV):       Hechos Relevantes / Otras informaciones
                    Thresholds: 3%, 5% and each 5%-multiple thereafter

Netherlands (AFM):  Substantial holdings register
                    Thresholds: 3%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 40%, 50%,
                                60%, 75%, 95%

Switzerland (SIX):  Significant Shareholders register
                    Thresholds: 3%, 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 33%, 50%, 66.6%

Sweden (Finansinspektionen): Major shareholdings register
                    Thresholds: 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%, 50%, 66.6%, 90%

Denmark (Finanstilsynet): Same EU thresholds

Norway (FIN):       Same EU thresholds plus 50% mandatory bid


CANADA — SEDAR+
---------------
Early Warning Report  >10% beneficial ownership (vs US 5%)
                      Filed within 1 business day
                      Equivalent to 13D in concept

Alternative Monthly Report (AMR)  Passive investors (similar to 13G)

Insider Reporting     SEDI - System for Electronic Disclosure by Insiders
                      Filed within 5 days

Form 62-103F1, 62-103F2, 62-103F3 (initial / monthly / change reports)


JAPAN — JFSA
------------
Large Shareholding Report (大量保有報告書)  >5% threshold
                                          Filed within 5 days
Change Report (変更報告書)                 1% change threshold

Tender Offer Disclosure                    Various thresholds


AUSTRALIA — ASIC
----------------
Substantial Holder Notice  >5% threshold (Form 603, 604, 605)
                           Filed within 2 business days
                           1% change reporting

Beneficial Ownership Tracing Notice  Section 672A inquiries


HONG KONG — HKEx + SFC
----------------------
Disclosure of Interest (DI) Notice  >5% threshold
                                    Filed within 3 business days
                                    Each 1% change above 5%


SINGAPORE — MAS + SGX
---------------------
Substantial Shareholder Notice  >5% threshold
                                Filed within 2 business days


INDIA — SEBI
------------
SAST (Substantial Acquisition of Shares and Takeovers) Regulations 2011
  - Disclosure at 5% threshold and 2% incremental changes

PIT (Prohibition of Insider Trading) Regulations
  - Insider trading disclosures within 2 days


BRAZIL — CVM
------------
CVM Instruction 358 / 567
  - >5% threshold disclosure
  - Filed within 5 days

DATA PULL WORKFLOW (per fund)
================================================================================

STEP 1: Identify fund identifier(s)
  - SEC CIK number (search EDGAR by name)
  - For non-US: equivalent regulator ID
  - Cross-reference Form ADV for related entities/funds

STEP 2: Pull base 13F (latest quarter)
  - Source: SEC EDGAR direct OR WhaleWisdom
  - Extract: ticker, shares, value, % of portfolio, change from prior

STEP 3: Pull 13F history (4-8 quarters back)
  - Identify: new positions, increases >25%, decreases, exits
  - Track: position size velocity, concentration trends

STEP 4: Pull 13D/G filings (last 12 months)
  - Source: SEC EDGAR + 13F.info + Bullishly
  - For each filing: extract Item 4 purpose, ownership %, target

STEP 5: Pull 13D/A amendments (any material change)
  - Triggers: ownership change, intent change, agreement amendments

STEP 6: Pull Form 4 (insider transactions if fund principal is insider)
  - Useful for fund-affiliated boards / Form 4 by manager personally

STEP 7: Pull recent investor letters (last 4 quarters)
  - Sources: fund website, ValueWalk, MOI Global, Substack archive
  - Extract: position rationale, conviction level, exits, additions

STEP 8: Search non-US filings if fund has international holdings
  - UK TR-1, AMF declarations, BaFin notifications, CONSOB filings
  - SEDAR+ for Canadian holdings

STEP 9: Cross-check insider trading at owned companies
  - OpenInsider for cluster buying patterns
  - Compare to fund's own additions

STEP 10: Document in spreadsheet
  - Tab per fund in fund_activity_last_6mo.xlsx
  - Standardized columns: Category (Conviction / Adds / New Inits / 5% Disclosure /
    Material Increase), Ticker, Detail, Source, Filing Date

GAP AREAS IN CURRENT METHODOLOGY (Areas to Improve)
================================================================================

1. **Non-US disclosure systematic coverage**
   Current pipeline relies on stockanalysis.com for foreign tickers but does
   NOT systematically pull TR-1 (UK), AMF (France), BaFin (Germany), CNMV
   (Spain), CONSOB (Italy), EDINET (Japan), HKEx, SGX, SEBI etc.
   FIX: Build per-jurisdiction agents to scan each regulator's disclosure feed
   on a weekly cadence; extract by fund name (e.g., TCI Fund Management,
   Cevian Capital, Petrus Advisers).

2. **13D/A amendments**
   Current process pulls initial 13Ds but may miss amendments that increase
   stakes incrementally.
   FIX: SEC EDGAR RSS feed monitoring by CIK; flag every 13D/A.

3. **Form 4 cluster detection**
   Insider buying clusters at fund-owned companies (CEO + CFO + 3 directors
   buying within a month) is a high-signal pattern not currently captured.
   FIX: OpenInsider cluster screen weekly; cross-reference against fund 13Fs.

4. **Investor letter capture**
   Currently anecdotal; many concentrated managers publish detailed letters
   that reveal conviction levels and thesis evolution.
   FIX: Build crawler for fund websites + ValueWalk + MOI Global; extract
   PDFs and run NLP for position mentions.

5. **Voting record / activist proposal tracking**
   Form NPX votes + DEF 14A proxy proposals reveal activist behavior.
   FIX: Quarterly NPX scrape; flag against shareholder proposal database.

6. **Sub-$1B microcap coverage**
   Many P5 investors operate sub-$1B where 13F filings are not required
   (managers below $100M AUM are exempt).
   FIX: Use Form ADV Part 1 (PF declarations) + state-level filings + Form D
   + forum signals (MicroCapClub, VIC) for these names.

7. **Short interest / borrow data**
   Yartseva framework doesn't use shorts but absent borrow signal misses
   activist short campaigns.
   FIX: S3 Partners + Ortex data feeds; cross-reference with 13F shorts.

8. **Options positioning**
   13Fs report puts/calls but most aggregators flatten to net long; option
   skew/IV data reveals informed positioning.
   FIX: CBOE COT-equivalent + OPRA tape sampling.

9. **Form 3/4/5 lag**
   Form 4 filings are within 2 days but EDGAR indexing has 1-2 day lag.
   FIX: SEC EDGAR XBRL feed real-time RSS for Form 4 by issuer + filer CIK.

10. **Trust / family office filings**
    Many wealthy individuals file via grantor trusts or limited partnerships
    that obscure beneficial ownership.
    FIX: Cross-reference EDGAR filers by SSN-equivalent identifiers; use
    DELAW.online / OpenCorporates for entity graph.

11. **PRTC (Pretrade Compliance) leaks**
    Block trades reported on TRACE / consolidated tape can pre-date 13F by
    weeks. Same with FINRA TRACE BB data.
    FIX: FINRA TRACE block trade reports for size signal.

12. **CFD / swap exposures**
    UK TR-1 captures CFDs but US 13F does not. Many hedge funds use swaps
    to maintain undisclosed exposure.
    FIX: Cross-reference UK TR-1 disclosures for fund holdings missing from
    US 13F. AlphaSense for swap-language extraction.
