WHY THIS LIST FILTERS FOR STALENESS-RESISTANT POSITIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

13F filings disclose holdings 45 days after quarter-end. For typical L/S hedge
funds turning over portfolios quarterly, this lag often makes the disclosure
already obsolete by the time it's public. But for several specific fund types,
staleness matters far less, and the signal can be acted on weeks or months later
without significant decay:

1. PM-OWNED FIRMS WITH PERMANENT CAPITAL ("eat own cooking")
   - No outside LP redemption pressure forces selling
   - Concentrated positions held for years, not quarters
   - PM's net worth IS the fund, so they don't churn

2. POST-RESTRUCTURING SPONSORS (received equity from a workout)
   - Cost basis is sunk; they hold until thesis plays out
   - Examples: Knighthead/HTZ, Mudrick/MNKTQ, Aurelius/CORZ

3. LONG-DURATION LAND, ROYALTY, OR REAL-ESTATE COMPOUNDERS
   - Decade-plus theses
   - Examples: TPL (Horizon Kinetics 40% / 25+ years), JOE (Berkowitz 80% /
     20+ years), NFGC (Sprott personal), MRP (multiple)

4. ACTIVIST CAMPAIGNS MID-FLIGHT (13D filed, board action pending)
   - The 13D is by definition recent (filed when crossing 5% w/ intent)
   - Catalyst path is public, dated, and observable

5. FAMILY OFFICES + CONTROL-STAKE HOLDERS (>=20% of co.)
   - Cannot exit quickly without disclosure
   - Routinely re-file SEDAR / TR-1 / 13D-A every few months

6. HYPER-CONCENTRATED SINGLE-NAME PM BETS (>=30% of portfolio)
   - The position IS the manager's career bet
   - They will not trim quietly between filings

SECTION 6: STALENESS FRAMEWORK SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

OK to act on a 3-6 month-old filing if:
  - Filer is SITG 5 (PM own money, no LP pressure)
  - Position is >=20% of company (cannot exit quickly)
  - Thesis has multi-year horizon (real estate, royalty, land bank)
  - Filer is post-BK sponsor (Knighthead, Mudrick, Aurelius, etc.)
  - Active 13D campaign or proxy fight underway
  - Family office vehicle (Mithaq, MILFAM, BLR Family Foundation)

Treat as stale (use only as context):
  - L/S hedge fund 13Fs from quant or multi-PM platforms
  - Tiger Cubs in <5% positions
  - Anyone with <2% concentration in their book
