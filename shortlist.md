# Shortlist — Best Opportunities

Applied output of the framework in `capital_structure_screening.md`. The
picks are ranked by triangulation strength (Leg 1: PF/through-cycle
valuation, Leg 2: cap-stack game theory, Leg 3: revealed preference) and
re-validated against the alignment scorecard.

**Caveats.** This is research synthesis from the documented universe, not
live data. Sizing requires real-time verification of (a) fulcrum-debt
prices, (b) latest Form 4 / PDMR / SEDI / SAST insider buys, (c) most
recent 13F / TR-1 / SAST institutional position changes, and (d) HY OAS
backdrop. Treat the picks as the *deal pipeline* the framework surfaces;
the actual entry decisions need a daily refresh.

**Bucket A · Score 22/28 · Triangulation 3/3**

- **Leg 1 (PF / through-cycle valuation).** NA frac trailing-10-year EBITDA
  percentiles ≈ C$35m / C$120m / C$240m (bear / base / bull). Peer-cohort

  backstop = relationship anchor. No DIP, no cramdown venue. Management
  pre-existing options underwater, not repriced.
- **Leg 3 (revealed preference).** Multiple director Form 4 buys at the
  rights price during the subscription window. Backstoppers took rump on

  Canadian insider entries at next reporting cycle.

**Trade.** Subscribe rights cum-cum; supplement via nil-paid rights
window if they clear 5–10% below TERP. Best seat = the rights themselves,
not the listed common ex-rights.

**Catalyst.** NA frac-cycle margin recovery — utilization and pricing
visible in next 1–2 quarterly prints. Producer 2026 capex revisions.

**Kill criteria.** Utilization <60% for two consecutive quarters; new
UCC-1 lien filed within 12 months of close; going-concern language
returns; director anchor sells.

## What to verify before sizing

The framework surfaces deal-pipeline candidates; final sizing requires a
daily refresh of:

1. **Fulcrum debt prices** (TRACE for US, Bloomberg / 9fin for foreign).
   If 2L trades at par, you missed the entry. If at distress, the
   bucket may need reclassification.
2. **Latest insider transactions** (Form 4 / PDMR / SEDI / SAST / ASX
   3Y) within last 30 days of subscription window.
3. **Most recent 13F / TR-1 / SAST** institutional changes — new value
   or recap-fund positions at recap pricing.
4. **HY OAS backdrop** (FRED `BAMLH0A0HYM2`) — sizing should scale with
   distress cycle, not narrative.
5. **Event-timeline stage** — most alpha lives in T = 0 dilution shock,
   nil-paid rights, and rump auction. Re-stage on every announcement.

universe. To surface fresh candidates, run weekly:

- **Form 4 ∩ Tier-S regex** within 30 days → confirms Leg 3
- **13D first-entry ∩ <50% of 5y high** → known recap-fund entry at
  distressed pricing (the Tenneco-shaped pattern)
- **Director rights take-up > pro-rata share** in DEF 14A / 8-K
- **Fulcrum-debt 50–70 ∩ listed-equity <10% of 5y peak** intersection
  in TRACE / market data
- **EBITDA 10th–25th-percentile cyclical screen** ∩ Tier-S signal in
  last 90 days

These five intersections are where new Calfrac / Exicom-class setups
appear.
