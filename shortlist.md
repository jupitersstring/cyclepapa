# Best Opportunities Today — Global Shortlist (May 2026 vintage)

Applied output of the framework in `capital_structure_screening.md`,
synthesizing the 2024–2026 recap vintage. Ranked by the four-question
gate:

1. *Rescue for whom?* (Bucket — §9)
2. *How good a deal is it?* (Scorecard — §2)
3. *Is it asymmetrically priced?* (Triangulation — §8; **alignment-gap
   ratio** is the headline number — §2.1 dim. 9)
4. *Which tranche, in which window?* (Fulcrum + Condition 7 — §4, §2.2)

Each candidate is tagged with:
- **Bucket** (A/B/C) — seat selection
- **Archetype** (A–G, §3.1) — deal mechanic
- **Gap** — alignment-gap ratio (anchor entry ÷ current market)
- **C7** — Condition-7 inflection status (visible / pending / unclear)

**Caveats.** Live verification needed for: current fulcrum-debt prices,
Form 4 / SEDI / PDMR / SAST / MAR Art. 19 insider transactions, most
recent 13F / TR-1 / EAR / SAST institutional changes, HY OAS backdrop,
and the specific stage of each deal's event timeline. Treat the ranking
as the *pipeline*; sizing requires the daily refresh.

## Pattern recognition — what's actually working in this cycle

The May 2026 vintage has produced a **distinctive French sovereign-
strategic playbook (Archetype A)** that is now the cleanest archetype in
the world: state development banks and strategic industrial anchors
write common equity at agreed prices that sit multiples above the
parallel rights-issue clearing price, creating a hard valuation floor
against which the public stock looks visibly mispriced. **Eutelsat and
Worldline are the textbook examples.**

This is structurally different from the **US Chapter 11 cycle**, which
has been **systematically hostile to legacy equity** (Spirit, Rite Aid,
Audacy, 2U, Enviva, Sunnova all delivered zero), and from the **Indian
IBC system**, which mandates equity wipeouts at confirmation. The
cleanest US plays therefore cluster either in **out-of-court LME where
old equity survived** (Hawaiian Electric, Lumen, iHeartMedia, Calumet,
Hycroft) or in the rare **post-reorg orphan with strategic anchor**
(Wolfspeed/Renesas).

The **Chinese property complex bifurcates sharply** on backstop quality:
Sunac (founder takes 23% of MCBs) and Sino-Ocean (state insurers retain
53.8%) cleanly satisfy alignment while Logan, Agile, Kaisa, and CIFI
lean creditor-takeover.

The **Malaysian PN17 system** has produced two genuinely aligned recaps
(Sapura via sovereign MoF and Capital A via Fernandes promoter).

The **Nigerian banking complex** is a once-in-a-cycle regulator-forced
recap basket where the entire sector has been recapitalised at
compliance multiples below book.

**The single most important pattern**: prior-cycle textbook winners have
already played out (Greek banks, Hanwha Ocean, Thai Airways, Patanjali
Foods, Saipem, Monte dei Paschi). The investable opportunity today is
concentrated in the new vintage — Eutelsat, Worldline, Vodafone Idea,
Sunac, Sapura, Hawaiian Electric, Star, Americanas, Sino-Ocean, Metro,
Synlait — where the recap is fresh enough that the equity has not yet
re-rated and the inflection catalyst has not yet hit. *The asymmetry
premium is being paid for the time gap between balance-sheet repair
(already done) and operational / regulatory inflection (still
pending)* — exactly the structural mispricing that defined Rolls-Royce
2020, ArcelorMittal 2016, and 3i 2009 in their respective cycles.
