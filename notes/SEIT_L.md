# SEIT.L

## Thesis

SDCL Efficiency Income Trust — committed managed wind-down at ~62% NAV
discount with Saba Capital accumulating (4 activist TR-1 buys for
+0.46pp combined). Renewables-DCF book so recovery rate 0.80, modest
path-risk haircut for power-price and discount-rate sensitivity.
Expected workout 28 months. The combination of confirmed activist
on the cap table + announced wind-down + 30pp wider than the
infrastructure sector median makes this the cleanest single-name
opportunity in the screen.

## Position

Target 5% of book. Build in three tranches around the post-announcement
anchored VWAP (currently roughly at VWAP — wait for any -3% gap below
to add to size).

## Exit

Sell into capital distributions as they're announced. Hard exit if
(a) Saba reduces stake materially without a counter-tender announced,
(b) NAV total return turns negative below -10% YoY, or (c) workout
slips beyond 36 months.
