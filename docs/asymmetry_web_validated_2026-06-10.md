# Web-Validated Asymmetric Opportunities — 2026-06-10

Stress-test of the quant `asymmetry` screen (top ~35 of 6,576 candidates over ~18,500 global
names) against current public information. Goal: keep only names with **real, persistent earnings
power + bounded downside + no disqualifier**, and kill the artifacts the fundamentals data cannot
see. Method: 5 parallel cluster-research agents (primary sources — SEC filings, company IR,
reputable press), then independent re-verification of the two most thesis-flipping facts.

Prices/caps are intraday-approximate as of 2026-06-09/10. "Fwd P/E / EV-EBITDA" from the screen
are noted where mechanically distorted (leverage, consolidation, one-offs, commodity base effects).

## TIER 1 — Cleanest asymmetry (own these first)

**1. AR — Antero Resources (US, nat-gas/NGL E&P, ~$11.0B)**
The single cleanest inflection in the gas group, and it's *organic*: Q1'26 adj FCF nearly tripled to
$658M driven by NGL/C3+ realizations (a structural differentiator, not a base effect), highest
LNG-fairway exposure in Appalachia (~2.3 Bcf/d to LNG points). The $2.8B HG Energy deal (closed
Feb'26) was funded with debt + divestiture + FCF — **no equity dilution** — and leverage stays
<1.0x. Multiple ways to win (gas strip + NGL/propane premium + LNG corridor). Only -5% drawdown,
~7.7x fwd P/E. *Kills it:* an NGL/propane price collapse (its edge cuts both ways).

**2. BAR.BR — Barco (Belgium, projection/medical displays, ~€693M)**
Downside is bounded by the balance sheet: **net cash ~€186M** (D/E ~6%). Yet it's growing — FY2025
EBITDA €125.1M (~13% margin, +4%), Entertainment +11%, €548M orderbook, **raised dividend (~6.5%
yield)**. Trades ~9.7x P/E, ~37% off highs on China/ClickShare sentiment, not balance-sheet stress.
You're paid to wait via the yield, protected by net cash, with cinema-projector replacement + medical
display ramp as the call option. *Kills it:* China healthcare stays weak and the H2-weighted recovery
fails to show.

**3. PRGS — Progress Software (US, infrastructure software, ~$1.4B)**
A 40%+-operating-margin recurring-revenue cash machine at ~5x fwd P/E. Q1'26 EPS $1.60 (+22%),
ARR ~$863M. The two overhangs are clearing: net leverage 3.1x → guided 2.7x by year-end, and the
**SEC closed its MOVEit investigation with no enforcement action**. Dividend trivially covered (the
aggregator "TTM $0.00" is a stale ex-date artifact, not a cut). *Kills it:* a large debt-funded deal
that breaks the deleveraging story, or ARR turning negative (roll-up out of road).

**4. KROS — Keros Therapeutics (US, biotech, ~$220–241M)**
Trades **below net cash** — $281.5M cash, no debt (31 Mar 2026) vs ~$220–241M cap — with an activist
(ADAR1) enforcing the floor: the strategic review already yielded a **$375M capital-return plan**.
The Q1'25 earnings "spike" was a Takeda license milestone (artifact, now lapped). You get the cash
discount + a free call option on the rinvatercept (KER-065) DMD Phase 2 starting Q2'26 and the
retained Takeda-partnered elritercept. *Kills it:* the board commits the cash to speculative R&D
instead of returning it. (Watch the June 2026 ADAR1/board update.)

**5. EXE — Expand Energy (US, nat-gas E&P, ~$22B)**
Largest US gas producer, and the asymmetry is a **self-help lever that pays regardless of the gas
strip**: synergies ~$400M captured in 2025 → ~$500M run-rate by YE2026, on top of a Q1'26 with $1.7B
FCF and rapid deleveraging (net debt $2.8B, down $1.6B). The -17% looks like gas-beta sentiment, not
company trouble; an acquirer, not a target. *Kills it:* Henry Hub strip rolls lower — it's still a
beta name once synergies are banked.

## Cluster (theme) verdicts

**(a) US natural-gas E&P (EXE/GPOR/CRK/AR/GRNT): thesis intact but a 2H27+ duration bet, not a
near-term strip story.** Demand pillars are real and confirmed (Golden Pass Train 1 first LNG
30-Mar-26; Plaquemines >2.8 Bcf/d; Corpus Christi Stage 3; data-center/AI gas demand). But EIA sees
Henry Hub flat-to-down 2026→2027 (~$3.50→$3.18 avg) because supply keeps pace, and a global LNG supply
wave is a 2026 bearish counter. So the 12-month underperformance reflects a flat front strip, not a
broken thesis. **Own the idiosyncratic levers (AR's liquids/LNG, EXE's synergies), not the
distressed/speculative cheap optics (CRK's cash burn, GRNT's declining EBITDA).**

**(b) BPO / IT-services / SaaS — is AI melting them? Bifurcated, not blanket.** AI is *enabling* the
adaptable (TaskUs AI-services +59%) and *deflating* commodity labor-arbitrage seats (TTEC: rev -7%,
thin equity vs $803M debt). In data SaaS, lock-in-light feeds melt (Definitive Healthcare: rev -6%,
goodwill written off twice) while scaled/embedded + FCF-rich names are merely *priced* for the fear
(ZoomInfo). Contractual/regulated/recurring revenue is holding (PRGS infra-software ARR, CBZ
accounting/advisory). **The "AI kills all BPO/data" screen-bear is too blunt.**

**(c) Busted biotechs below net cash: KROS yes, PLRX yes-but-lottery, AGEN no.** KROS (~30%/15-25%
below cash + activist + capital return) and PLRX (~43% of net cash) clear the below-cash bar with
live catalysts; **AGEN fails outright** — trades *above* cash (~$35M vs ~$146M), explicit
going-concern doubt, prior 1-for-20 reverse split, perpetual ATM dilution. ZEAL is a quality obesity
name but *priced* (the +146% rev was a Roche milestone, not organic).

## What the web changed vs the raw screen (the meta-lesson)

The screen's blind spots, in order of damage:
1. **Restructuring / dilution** — the "cheap EV/EBITDA" is a debt-and-dilution illusion once equity is
   wiped (Casino, Intrum, ABO, Fasadgruppen). The single biggest source of false positives.
2. **Acquisition-driven "inflections"** read as organic growth (Xerox/Lexmark, CBIZ/Marcum,
   Immersion/BNED consolidation).
3. **Commodity base effects & MTM** (Gulfport post-bankruptcy, Comstock hedge marks, Mosaic).
4. **Milestone artifacts** in biotech revenue (Zealand/Roche, Keros/Takeda).
5. **Stale data masking a live blow-up** (Verra Mobility's Avis termination; Grieg's ex-dividend gap).

The survivors share a common shape: **bounded downside** (net cash — Barco, Keros, Pliant; or a
self-help lever independent of the cycle — Expand synergies, Progress recurring ARR; or quality —
Cigna), **multiple independent ways to win**, and **earnings that are real and persistent** rather
than mechanical. That is the actual definition of asymmetry the quant score only approximates.
