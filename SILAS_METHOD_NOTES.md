## 2. Chart-selection nuance for single equities

This is where her practice is most specific, and most of it generalises directly
to any IPO-chart scanner:

1. **First-trade (IPO) chart trumps incorporation chart for trading.** Both
   exist and tell different stories; "you can only trade the markets with one"
   (Meta post). The incorporation chart shows internal/corporate events that
   "the public may never know about." V1 teaches the distinction.
2. **Rebrand / ticker change / index migration = new chart.** Facebook→Meta
   ($FB→$MVRS, first trade 1 Dec 2021) required discarding all pending eclipse
   hits on the old chart and starting fresh. She treats index moves the same
   way in kind if not degree (Tesla "eclipsed off the Nasdaq" to the S&P; her
   Tesla manual is built on that transition).
3. **Uncertain birth data is resolved empirically.** For Bitcoin she rejects
   the Jan 3 genesis chart because backtested transits "don't gel," and adopts
   a Satoshi forum-announcement chart (London, guessed location) because exact
   transit hits validate it: **"For traders the data CANNOT BE WRONG."**
   Chart validity = hit-rate of exact transits against known price history.
4. **CEO/founder synastry overlays the company chart.** Musk↔Tesla,
   Wood↔ARKK, Zuckerberg↔Facebook, Buffett↔Berkshire (March 2020): "a CEO's
   personal transits often lend to the performance of a financial company."
   With unknown birth times she uses a 12:00 electional chart and flags reduced
   confidence.
5. **Angles matter and need a real first-trade time.** The MC is "the business
   point" of an IPO chart; eclipse/planet hits to MC/ASC are first-class events
   (Apple: May 2022 lunar eclipse on IPO MC within 1° + conjunct natal Uranus).

## 3. The eclipse hierarchy (her core single-equity doctrine)

- **"An eclipse eclipses all other transits."** Eclipses "always take priority
  over the every day of planets transiting the sky." Never issue a confident
  directional forecast against a pending eclipse to the chart; never hold a
  heavy position through one unknowingly. Course copy: "Eclipses — the number
  one thing that moves equities in a big way."
- **Eclipse to the IPO Sun is the master signal.** "IPO Sun eclipses are the
  most important driver of price and stories to companies."
  - Solar eclipse **conjunct** Sun → power/boost, effects 6-12 months, changes
    "go for years," irreversible ("they are final").
  - Eclipse **opposite** Sun → power drain / difficulty for ~6-12 months, and
    typically "a nice dip on price ahead of what changes may be in store."
  - Polarity matters: conjunction and opposition are NOT interchangeable.
  - Lunar vs solar: lunar = endings/shorter thematic arc; solar = bigger,
    longer, more structural.
- **"Eclipsed out of existence": her M&A/delisting/rebrand detector.** An
  eclipse on the Sun, with corroborating factors, flags takeover, merger,
  bankruptcy or rebrand (Facebook→Meta happened 3 weeks after she flagged 14
  eclipses stacking on Zuckerberg + the Nov 2021 lunar on both Suns; Twitter:
  Dorsey resigned on the 27° Taurus lunar opposite the IPO Sun, Musk arrived
  on the follow-through). Explicitly "not an every-instance scenario" — needs
  a series of other factors and company circumstances.
- **Timing windows (verbatim rules):**
  - News can show up "one month before or even one month after" the eclipse
    date (unless a transit "sets it off" earlier).
  - Once activated, "give it 6 weeks to 3 months roughly" on price; "6 weeks
    for traders is a good model to use."
  - Position "about six weeks ahead" of the eclipse (GME April 2024 eclipse).
  - Lunar-eclipse trades need "6 months time frame to play out safely" (NVDA).
  - "Never rely on an exact date — there is always a window on either side."
- **Orb:** within 1° = "a tight placement" (her strongest hits are ≤1°:
  GME eclipse 19° Aries vs IPO Mars 18° Aries "almost exact"; Apple eclipse on
  MC "within one degree").
- **Eclipse-to-Uranus = wildcard.** Direction cannot be biased; "expect the
  unexpected"; use stops; avoid options unless deliberately taking the casino
  side; go read the news for the sector theme (Uranus = tech/crypto).
- **Eclipses are triggered.** A later transit over the eclipse degree (Mars is
  her usual culprit — "Mars was at 19 degrees Gemini - a common trigger")
  detonates the stored eclipse; a "good transit to the chart often sets it off."
- **Eclipse series = a story.** Consecutive eclipses are "chapters"; the Sun
  eclipse is "the main story and cover title." She reads series, not events.
- **Saros/series rhyming for macro context.** Maps current eclipse degrees to
  prior same-sign series (1998 LTCM/Russia, 2007-09 GFC) and expects thematic
  repeats; notes markets can rally into visible disaster (Nasdaq +40% in 1998,
  S&P record highs in Oct 2007) before the eclipse-flagged break.

## 4. Transit doctrine for single equities

- **Confluence rule: "it takes 3 or more things to make something happen."**
  Multiple confirming transits raise conviction, "but an eclipse takes top
  priority."
- **Pluto conjunct IPO Sun = premier growth/power transit** (her NVDA call:
  "explains the literal power the company currently has in the marketplace").
  It is "a GROWTH transit not a one day wonder."
- **Stations and multi-pass logic are load-bearing.** A retrograding planet
  crosses a natal point three times; the transit is not "finished" until the
  final pass ("Pluto will be back over Neptune and the Sun January and
  February 2025 ... to finish off the transit for good"). While a heavy planet
  is *stationing* on a natal point, do not short — "any sort of news could
  come from the blue." Momentum decays only as the aspect **separates**:
  "Public momentum wore off as the astrology did" (GME $60→$19).
- **Named red flags (company charts):** Uranus square Sun ("a big red flag"),
  Uranus square Uranus ("disruptive"), Saturn square Pluto ("another red
  flag"). Personal/CEO charts: Jupiter opposite Pluto ("one of the most
  financially non-beneficial transits"), Jupiter square Saturn (bad for money),
  Pluto square Moon (mental drain — trade lighter).
- **Named positives:** Jupiter conjunct Uranus within 2° ("a lucky
  combination"); Jupiter/Venus/Uranus/Sun stellium in Taurus ("the sign of
  money and share markets" → speculative frenzy); Pluto conjunct IPO Mercury
  (news flow); Uranus trine Sun/Mercury; Jupiter trine Neptune = short-lived
  euphoric pop ("one day wonder"); Mars conjunct natal Mars can mark exact
  swing lows (GME bottom 24-25 May 2024).
- **The absence read.** No major Pluto/Uranus/Saturn hard transits into 2027 on
  NVDA → "it would be purely technical to short this one ... not astrology."
  A clean forward stack is itself a signal (no astrological short case).
- **Direction is the hard part; time is the deliverable.** She gauges *when*
  "a price event" is due, then uses technicals, news ("Cluedo"), positioning
  data (unusual options flow before the Musk/Twitter reveal) and sentiment to
  guess *which way*.

Already aligned with her practice:

- Eclipse-forward hits weighted above stations/progressions; Sun multiplier
  1.5x — direction of her hierarchy, weaker than her "master transit" weight.

  tech/crypto matches the modern tier).

Gaps her nuance suggests (concrete, implementable):
1. **Polarity split on eclipse-to-Sun hits**: conjunction (power, bullish
   years-long) vs opposition (drain, dip-first) — we currently score conj/opp/
   square identically. Add signed subtype to fwd_hits.
2. **Tight-orb kicker at ≤1°** on eclipse hits (her "tight placement"), on top
   of the linear 2° falloff.
3. **Eclipse hits to IPO MC/ASC** — we score natal angularity but do not test
   forward eclipses against ASC/MC. Add ASC/MC to score_forward targets
   (accepting the 9:30 assumed-time caveat; she would insist on a verified
   first-trade time before trusting angle hits).
4. **"Eclipsed-out" M&A flag**: eclipse conj/opp natal Sun within 1° in the
   forward window → tag ECLIPSE_SUN_EVENT as a takeover/rebrand/delisting
   candidate signal, separate from the bullish score.
5. **Position-by date**: first qualifying eclipse hit minus 6 weeks, per her
   positioning rule; and a 6-week-to-3-month decay window after each eclipse
   hit instead of a point event.
6. **Multi-pass transit modelling**: track first/second/final passes of
   Pluto/Neptune/Uranus over natal Sun/Mercury; suppress "decay" classification
   until the final pass separates (her NVDA logic).
7. **McWhirter node gate**: add North Node sign (esp. Pisces→Aquarius July
   2026 = cycle bottom) as a macro dampener alongside BARBAULT — she and our
   BARBAULT table disagree about 2026-2030 polarity in instructive ways (she
   is bearish into mid-2026 and 2028; BARBAULT calls 2024-2030 RISING).
8. **Triggered-eclipse timing**: score a secondary date when transiting Mars
   crosses a stored eclipse degree that aspects the natal chart.

Items 1-2 and 4-5 are cheap (score_forward changes only); 3 needs the angle
