# Universe — Broad Candidate List

The watchlist that feeds the framework. Organized by region → sector →
bucket. Three confidence tiers:

- **★ Confirmed** — recap event documented in this repo or widely reported
- **○ Probable** — recap event known from public reporting; mechanism
  needs verification before scoring
- **▲ Watch** — sector under stress; specific catalyst not yet announced
  but a Tier-S filing is plausible in next 6–12 months

Bucket tags use the §9 framework: **A** = listed common is the trade,
**B** = anchor instrument is the trade, **C** = legacy cancelled, new
common is a separate trade.

> **This is a research watchlist, not investment recommendations.** Many
> names need live verification (current cap stack, fulcrum debt prices,
> Form 4 / SEDI / PDMR / SAST insider activity, latest 13F / TR-1
> changes) before sizing.

## Key takeaways

- The documented universe (~25 names in `capital_structure_screening.md`)
  was a starting point; the real working universe is **150–200 active
  candidates** plus a sector-watch list that surfaces 5–15 new names per
  month in stress regimes.
- Within each sector, expect the bucket distribution to be roughly
  20% Bucket A (listed common is the trade) / 10% Bucket B (anchor
  instrument) / 70% Bucket C (legacy cancelled). The framework's job
  is to find the 20%.
- The "fishing grounds" list is where you spend the §1 pipeline budget.
  Setting up 200–500 saved EDGAR / SEDAR+ / RNS / SEDI / PDMR alerts
  against these sectors is the discovery infrastructure.
- New names enter the universe weekly via the §1.7 regex + §1.5 insider
  feed intersection. The shortlist (`shortlist.md`) is the *output* of
  the pipeline; this universe is the *input*.
