## Prevailing valuation — read on the cohort

The most important read across this list is that **balance sheets have
been repaired but operating multiples have not re-rated**. The cohort
sits at a structural mispricing in the time gap between recap completion
(visible to investors) and operational inflection (still pending). This
is exactly the setup that defined Rolls-Royce 2020 → 2024 (10×), 3i
Group 2009 → 2014 (5×), ING 2009 → 2012 (5×), and ArcelorMittal 2016 →
2021 (5×).

Position size should scale with how early the name is on this curve.

   The richest asymmetry. Eutelsat, Worldline, Vodafone Idea, Sunac,
   Kaisa, CIFI, Solocal, Sapura, Lithium Americas, Lynas, Patriot

### Three valuation themes that define this vintage

1. **Sovereign-anchored critical minerals create a hard valuation floor**
   that the market has not yet repriced. MP Materials' DoD $110/kg NdPr
   floor — *twice spot at deal date* — is real downside protection
   that no commercial counterparty would write. Algoma Steel's CEEFC
   warrants at C$11.08 + 0%-spread loan, Lithium Americas' 24-year
   0%-spread DOE loan, Vulcan's EIB facility — all create cost-of-
   capital arbitrages that the listed equity has not priced in. The
   trade is buying the *equity attached to subsidised capital*.

2. **The French sovereign-strategic playbook (Eutelsat, Worldline)
   prices listed equity at multiples below the anchor's entry price.**
   When the French State, Bharti, UK Government, CMA CGM, Bpifrance,
   Crédit Agricole, and BNP Paribas all write equity cheques at €4.00
   and €2.75 while the rights clear at €1.35 and €0.20, the asymmetry
   is mechanical — anchors will not sell below their cost basis through
   the lockup, but the listed common is unattached to that floor and
   trades on dilution math. Buying the listed common is buying the
   *same instrument the sovereign bought, cheaper*.

3. **Founder/family alignment on BOTH sides of the cap stack (Mountain
   Province / Desmond) is the cleanest possible setup** — the fulcrum
   conflict that breaks most recaps doesn't exist because one party
   owns both the debt and the equity. Sunac's founder taking 23% of
   the MCBs with a 6-year lock-up, Kaisa's Kwok pledging the shareholder
   loan and reserving 20% MIP at recap, the 3G trio injecting R$12bn
   into Americanas, the Lin family converting their shareholder loan
   in CIFI, Lévy/Niel taking 64.8% of Solocal — these are textbook
   condition-#9 alignment signals that historically precede multibagger
   re-rates.

### Macro context

HY OAS regime today is mid-cycle. Default rates are normalising. The
**French sovereign-strategic playbook is unique to the 2024–2026 vintage**
— it has no clear post-war historical precedent at this scale across
multiple national champions. The **Chinese property MCB cascade**
($55bn+ offshore debt cleared via founder-anchored schemes) is the
largest coordinated EM corporate restructuring in history. The
**DoD/DoE/EIB critical-minerals industrial-policy anchor** is a 2024
policy innovation that is still in its first generation of deals.

The investable opportunity sits in the time gap between balance-sheet
repair (already done across the entire cohort) and operational /
regulatory inflection (still pending in 80% of the list). That gap is
the asymmetry premium — exactly the structural mispricing that defined
Rolls-Royce 2020, 3i 2009, ArcelorMittal 2016, Charter 2009, ING 2009,
and the LATAM Airlines 2022 re-listing in their respective cycles.
