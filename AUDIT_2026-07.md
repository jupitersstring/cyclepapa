# Cyclepapa Pipeline Audit — July 2026

Scope: data collection, analysis/scoring, ranking, thematic work, dating, plus
integrity, architecture, and backtesting. Every finding below is backed by a
query or `file:line` against the live repo.

---

## Executive summary

The pipeline is **broad and well-instrumented on plumbing** (coverage, guards for
past bug classes, snapshots, freshness of the event feeds), but has three
**structural** weaknesses that limit how much the output can be trusted as *alpha*
rather than *description*:

1. **The empirical foundation is hollow.** The whole score is validated by a
   backtest of **19 events**; several `base_rates` are computed on **n = 0–2**
   samples (two are fabricated `0.5` defaults). Nothing tests the composite
   score's predictive power out-of-sample. We are ranking on hand-tuned weights,
   not measured edge.
2. **The composite score is an uncalibrated linear+log blend dominated by one
   curated term.** `s1_top_pick = 2·s1` is uncapped and linear, so mega-caps win
   on curated-sheet popularity (AMZN's `s1`=70 of a 171 score) while the
   expensive conviction-weighted 13F term contributes ~8. Ranking ≈ "who's in the
   most curated top-pick lists."
3. **A live CUSIP mis-mapping corrupts signals right now.** The 13F name→ticker
   mapper collapses whole issuer families onto one operating ticker — every
   Invesco ETF → IVZ, every "Global*" issuer → GLP (score 34.6, smart-money 15.5,
   **entirely from 137 mis-mapped ETF holdings**). 30 "common" tickers are
   contaminated.

Plus the dating reality you flagged: **13F "smart money" is ~3.5 months old**
(64 days stale + the statutory quarter lag) and nothing surfaces that.

Below, ranked by (decision impact × correctness risk) ÷ effort.

## Tier 1 — Critical (correctness / validity)

### 1. CUSIP family-collapse mis-mapping inflates real tickers  ·  effort: M
- **Now:** `fund_13f_holdings` maps 198 distinct CUSIPs to `IVZ`, 71 to `WT`, 71
  to `GLP` — the name→ticker matcher in `ingest_13f.py` (`name_to_ticker`) prefix-
  matches ETF/fund issuers ("GLOBAL X FDS", "INVESCO EXCH TRD") onto the operating
  company that shares a name stem. 1,240 tickers draw from >1 CUSIP; **30 "common"
  tickers receive ETF-issuer holdings.**
- **Impact:** GLP shows `smart_money_n=15.5, score=34.6` built on 137 phantom
  holders; IVZ 302 ETF-ish holdings. These names rank on garbage, and their
  holder counts pollute consensus/`smart_money`.
- **Fix:** Map by **CUSIP→ticker via the SEC CUSIP-to-ticker/`company_tickers`
  authority, not name-stem matching.** Reject issuers whose CUSIP isn't a 1:1
  match; flag any ticker receiving >1 CUSIP for review. Add an invariant test:
  no operating ticker may receive holdings whose issuer matches
  `ETF|FUND|TRUST|SHARES|EXCH TRD`.

### 2. The backtest can't support the weights it's used to justify  ·  effort: L
- **Now:** `backtest_events` = 19 rows / 6 buckets; `base_rates` sample sizes are
  {0, 0, 1, 2, 2, 2, 5, 7}. `insider_cluster` = "hit_rate 1.0" on **n=2**;
  `sponsor_anchor`/`family_anchor` carry `0.5 / 0.17` on **n=0** (defaults, not
  measurements). These feed `expected_return.weighted_excess_12m` → the score.
- **Impact:** Every claim that a signal "works" rests on ≤7 samples. No
  out-of-sample or cross-sectional test of the composite score exists.
- **Fix:** Build a **real point-in-time backtest**: snapshot `unified_signal` each
  quarter (or reconstruct from filing dates), compute forward 6/12-month excess
  return per score decile, and report IC / decile spread. Until there's ≥ ~100
  events/bucket, treat `base_rates` as priors, not truth, and **stop feeding
  `expected_return` into the live score** (see #9).

### 3. Score is an uncalibrated blend dominated by the curated term  ·  effort: M
- **Now** (`unified_score.py:381-440`): linear uncapped terms (`s1_top=2·s1`,
  `s3=3·s3`, `s4=1.5·s4`, `cluster_pct_book=1.5·n5_pb`) are summed with log terms
  (`smart_money=2·log1p(n)`, `insider_dollars`, `form4`). Top names: AMZN `s1=70`,
  MSFT `s1=72`, NVDA `s1=74` — the section-1 curated count *is* the ranking.
- **Impact:** The ranking is a popularity contest that surfaces mega-caps; the
  conviction-weighting, valuation, and catalyst work is drowned out. Weights are
  hand-set ("adversarial review"), not fit to returns.
- **Fix:** Put terms on a **common scale** — z-score or percentile-rank each
  component cross-sectionally, then weight; or cap `s1`/`s3`/`s4` and log-compress
  counts. Publish a per-term contribution breakdown (you already store
  `components`) and calibrate weights against #2's forward returns.

### 4. 13F "smart money" is ~3.5 months stale and unflagged  ·  effort: S
- **Now:** latest 13F `filed = 2026-05-18` (64d ago) and those are **Q1 as-of
  2026-03-31** holdings (statutory +45d lag). The workbooks present
  `smart_money_n` as current. Q2 13Fs (as-of 6/30) file ~Aug 14 — not yet in.
- **Impact:** The headline signal reflects March positions; a fund could have
  fully exited. Mixing today's `ticker_yf` mcap/price with Q1 holdings in
  `pct_book`/guards is a mild look-ahead.
- **Fix:** Store and display an **as-of date per signal** ("13F as-of 2026-03-31")
  in the workbook headers; add a freshness banner. Weight 13F conviction by
  recency of the filing. Don't imply currency the data doesn't have.

## Tier 2 — High (coverage / robustness / process)

### 5. 36% of the roster produces zero holdings  ·  effort: M
- **Now:** `fund_meta`=452 but only **292** funds have 13F holdings; **162 have
  none.** The bottleneck is the hardcoded `FUND_CIK` dict in `ingest_13f.py` +
  incomplete CIK resolution (`fund_resolution_state`=430 rows).
- **Fix:** Move `FUND_CIK` to a data file; run `resolve_fund_ciks.py` to
  completion for the 162 unresolved, verify each against EDGAR 13F filings, and
  report the residual truly-non-filing funds (foreign/SMA) explicitly rather than
  silently carrying them.

### 6. No orchestration, no tests, stale Makefile  ·  effort: M
- **Now:** the real pipeline is ~20 hand-run steps; `make refresh` references
  **none** of `ingest_13f`, `enrich_yfinance`, `unified_score`,
  `render_universe_sheet`, `render_style_workbook`, or `render_people_monitor` —
  it still calls the retired `render_workbook`. No `tests/`, no CI.
- **Impact:** Order is tribal knowledge (as this session's manual reruns show);
  a missed step silently ships stale numbers.
- **Fix:** One `pipeline/run_all.py` DAG (ingest → enrich → score → derive →
  render → snapshot) that's idempotent and resumable; a `make refresh` that
  matches it; a `tests/test_invariants.py` run in CI (see #8).

### 7. Ranking isn't comparable across size buckets  ·  effort: M
- **Now:** Top-50 (common) is **44% large-cap** (22/50) despite `micro_bonus`.
  There's no size/sector neutralization — a mega-cap's raw holder count beats a
  micro-cap's conviction.
- **Fix:** Rank **within** `mcap_bucket` (and optionally sector), or convert score
  to a cross-sectional percentile; surface a "best-in-bucket" view so small-cap
  edge isn't buried under mechanical mega-cap scores.

### 8. Guards are read-time filters, not stored corrections  ·  effort: S
- **Now:** invariant sweep finds **7 holdings with value > 1.5× issuer mcap** and
  **2 Form 4 rows priced > $200k** still in the base tables — the guards live in
  `unified_score.py` / `cluster_detector.py` / `scan_insider_sharded.py` /
  `render_*`, so raw violations persist and any new consumer that forgets a guard
  re-introduces the bug (this exact thing happened twice already).
- **Fix:** A single `pipeline/validate.py` that (a) runs all invariants and fails
  loudly, and (b) quarantines/flags violating rows at ingest. Centralize the
  guard predicates in one module both ingest and scoring import.

## Tier 3 — Medium (methodology / hygiene)

### 9. `expected_return` is negligible and unsound  ·  effort: S
- Covers **14 of 8,937** scored tickers, derived from the 19-event backtest, yet
  wired into every score (`er_contribution = er_pct·0.5`, `unified_score.py:400`).
  **Remove from the live score** until #2 gives it a real basis; keep as a
  research column.

### 10. Thematic `macro_style` is a lopsided manual rollup  ·  effort: M
- `fund_style.sub_group → macro_style` is a hand-curated 2-level map; two
  categories hold **1 fund** ("PE/SPAC/Gold/Mining", "Macro/Trend"), "Other" 11.
  No validation that a fund's style matches its actual holdings. **Fix:** derive a
  data-driven style signature from holdings (sector/turnover/concentration/mcap
  tilt) and reconcile against the manual label; collapse the singleton buckets.

### 11. Single-source, partially-stale market data  ·  effort: M
- All valuation is Yahoo (`ticker_yf`); `prices` is **33d stale** (last 6/18) and
  covers ~815 tickers. **Fix:** add a second reference (Stooq/Tiingo/FMP) for
  cross-checks and fill; refresh `prices` in the DAG; widen price history to the
  ranked universe so `entry_intact` isn't yfinance-only.

### 12. Cruft: dead scripts + leftover table  ·  effort: S
- ~25 one-off scripts (`forensic_*`, `aggressive_buy_signals`, `secular_tailwind_
  filter`, `rerank_*`, `multibag_*`, `seed_*`, `fix_13f_master`, `close_all_gaps`)
  obscure the ~20 live ones; `ticker_yf__old` temp table still in the DB. **Fix:**
  move one-offs to `pipeline/oneoff/`, drop `ticker_yf__old`, document the live set.

### 13. Verify amendment & share-type handling  ·  effort: S
- Confirm 13F-HR/A **amendments** supersede (not duplicate) originals, that
  `sh_type` PUT/CALL options are excluded from long conviction (they're in the
  table), and that 13D/**A** amendments update the stake. Add counts to
  `validate.py`.

## Dating — consolidated (your explicit concern)

| Feed | Latest | Real staleness | Issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13F holdings | 2026-05-18 | **64d + Q1 as-of** (~3.5mo) | presented as current (#4) |
| prices | 2026-06-18 | 33d | not in refresh loop (#11) |
| edgar_filings | 2026-06-17 | 34d | scan cadence |
| Form 4 / 13D / 8-K | 2026-07-10 | 11d | good |
| ticker_yf | 2026-07-12 | 9d | good |

Principles to adopt: (a) **every signal carries an as-of date** and the workbook
shows it; (b) **never mix as-of dates silently** — today's price vs Q1 holdings
must be labeled; (c) **guard against look-ahead** in the backtest by entering at
filing-date+1, not event-date; (d) survivorship — delisted names (`yf_dead`, 1,349)
must stay in historical backtests, not be dropped.
