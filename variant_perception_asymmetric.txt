HOW TO READ THIS FILE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Asymmetric return comes from variant perception that turns out correct.
"3x upside / 0.3x downside = 10:1" is a static framework. The dynamic
framework is: what does the smart-money holder know or believe that the
marginal price-setter doesn't?

The most asymmetric setups are where:
  (a) the variant view is plausible (not crazy),
  (b) the holder is concentrated enough that being right matters to them,
  (c) the catalyst path forces the market to revalue.

Below are 20 setups ranked by clarity-of-variant-perception. The bigger
the gap between market belief and smart-money belief, the more asymmetric
the payoff if the variant view is correct.

THE VARIANT-PERCEPTION TYPOLOGY
================================================================================

The 20 setups above cluster into 7 types of variant perception. Knowing
the type helps you size and time the position:


TYPE A: UNDERAPPRECIATED PERMANENT CAPITAL THESIS
  LOGC (Abrams), PSUS (Ackman), HHH (Ackman), ARTV (RA Capital concentration)

  Common pattern: a disciplined operator has acquired control of a public
  vehicle and is now executing a 5-10 year build. Market hasn't yet
  caught up to the structural change. Catalyst: each new acquisition /
  announcement re-rates incrementally.

  POSITION SIZE: Core (3-6% of book each)
  HORIZON: 5-15 years


TYPE B: HIDDEN ASSET / SUM-OF-PARTS
  MASI (consumer audio split), JOE (entitled land NAV), TPL (royalty)
  HHH (real estate + Ackman strategy)

  Common pattern: stated EBITDA + cashflow understates the asset's
  intrinsic value because (a) accounting hides the asset, (b) it's not
  yet productive, or (c) the operating company structure has a natural
  split point.

  POSITION SIZE: Core (4-8% of book each)
  HORIZON: 3-10 years


TYPE C: SECTOR HATRED MISPRICED
  HYMC (gold restart), Galantas (gold), TFPM (gold), CORZ (crypto/AI)

  Common pattern: an entire sector is out of favor. The smart-money
  buyer is positioned BEFORE the sector reflexively returns. Timing
  matters more here than in other types.

  POSITION SIZE: 1-3% per name; total sector exposure 5-15%
  HORIZON: 18-48 months (gold cycle), 12-24 months (crypto)


TYPE D: CONTROL-STAKE BUYER OF LAST RESORT
  PLCE (Mithaq 61%), NEXN (Mithaq 30%), NETD (Funicular 56%),
  Berkowitz/JOE 80% of own fund

  Common pattern: smart-money holder is at/near control. They literally
  cannot exit without revealing exit intent. The position size IS the
  thesis. Cannot trade quietly; cannot drop without disclosure.

  POSITION SIZE: 2-5% per name
  HORIZON: 3-7 years


TYPE E: ACTIVIST-FORCED CHANGE PENDING
  MAT (Hawkins letter), MASI (Politan won), WEX (Impactive settled),
  SEER (Radoff buyout), BRN (Radoff escalation)

  Common pattern: catalyst is a dated, public, observable corporate
  action — proxy vote, board decision, AGM, settlement. The variant
  view is that the action will happen and will be value-accretive.

  POSITION SIZE: 2-4% per name
  HORIZON: 6-18 months


TYPE F: POST-RESTRUCTURING EQUITY TAIL
  MNKTQ (Mallinckrodt), HTZ (Hertz), CORZ, VRM (Vroom), BALY (Bally's),
  LTM (LATAM)

  Common pattern: equity emerged from bankruptcy or major restructure
  with sponsor still holding cost basis. Cashflow recovers; equity tail
  re-rates 3-6x as creditworthiness improves.

  POSITION SIZE: 1-3% per name
  HORIZON: 12-36 months


TYPE G: CROSS-FUND CONSENSUS, UNCROWDED
  REZI (3 funds), RAPT (3 biotech specialists), NKTR (3 biotech),
  MICC (2 deep-value buyers), LPX (3 concentrated value)

  Common pattern: multiple SITG funds independently arrived at the same
  conclusion in the same quarter. Cross-fund consensus is a strong
  signal because the funds typically have different mandates and
  diligence processes.

  POSITION SIZE: 1-3% per name
  HORIZON: 12-24 months


================================================================================
SIZING / EXECUTION DISCIPLINE
================================================================================

Rules of thumb for variant-perception sizing:

  STRENGTH OF VARIANT × CONCENTRATION OF SMART MONEY × CATALYST CLARITY
  = position size weight

  Strongest combo: VARIANT clear + concentration >30% + dated catalyst
    -> 4-6% position (LOGC, MASI, JOE, TPL, ARTV)

  Strong but cyclical: VARIANT clear + concentration >30% + catalyst
    diffuse -> 2-4% position (HYMC, TFPM, NETD, PLCE, NEXN)

  Activist proxy: VARIANT clear + dated board action
    -> 2-3% position (MAT, SEER, WEX, BRN, MNRO)

  Cross-fund consensus, smaller: -> 1-2% position
    (REZI, RAPT, NKTR, MICC, LPX)

  Convex warrants: -> 0.5-1% each, 3-5% total sleeve
    (Galantas, HYMC warrants, Blackrock Silver, MAXX, Westward)

  Post-BK equity tail: -> 1-2% each (illiquid)
    (MNKTQ, VRM, BALY, LTM)


================================================================================
WHAT KILLS VARIANT-PERCEPTION TRADES
================================================================================

Three failure modes specifically attack variant-perception setups:

  1. THE VARIANT VIEW BECOMES CONSENSUS WITHOUT DRIVING PRICE
     Example: market accepts that Mithaq is rebuilding PLCE, but cycle
     headwinds keep the multiple flat. Catalyst happens, no rerate.
     MITIGATION: position only at clear discount to "consensus-correct"
     fair value, not at peak optimism.

  2. THE SMART-MONEY HOLDER EXITS QUIETLY
     Example: Berkowitz starts trimming JOE more aggressively despite
     unchanged narrative. Read the 13D/A amendments carefully.
     MITIGATION: review every 13D/A and 13F for the holder; exit if
     they reduce >30% without thesis change.

  3. CATALYST FAILS DECISIVELY
     Example: Phase 2 readout misses for ARTV. Mattel board rejects
     Hawkins's strategic review. Vote fails for VSCO.
     MITIGATION: pre-define the failure event and your exit rule. Don't
     average down on terminal events.
