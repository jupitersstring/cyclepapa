QUALITATIVE DIMENSIONS NOT FULLY CAPTURED BY YARTSEVA / 13F SCREENS
================================================================================

The pure 9-dimensional thesis scoring from prior deep-dives captures:
  - Business model & moat
  - Operational data
  - Catalysts with dates
  - Variant perception
  - Downside floor
  - Upside path
  - Key risks
  - Insider/fund activity
  - Sector dynamics

But it under-weights or misses:

  1. MANAGEMENT QUALITY TRACK RECORD
     - Years of CEO tenure + capital allocation history
     - Prior compounding rate as a public-company executive
     - Communication consistency in investor letters

  2. MOAT DURABILITY (vs current strength)
     - Switching costs (high vs low)
     - Network effects (genuine vs claimed)
     - Brand pricing power (concrete examples)
     - Regulatory/IP moats with expiration timeline

  3. OWNER-OPERATOR ALIGNMENT
     - Founder still holds >2-3% (skin-in-game)
     - Compensation structure (long-term equity vs short-term cash)
     - Director purchases at depressed prices (vs comp-related)

  4. INDUSTRY POSITIONING
     - Is the company the #1 or #2 in a niche, or a sub-scale player?
     - Distribution / channel strength
     - Customer concentration vs diversification

  5. RECEPTOR-OF-CAPITAL DYNAMICS
     - Can the company actually deploy buybacks/dividends at scale?
     - Working capital structure (asset-light vs heavy)
     - M&A optionality (would a strategic buyer pay 2x what market does?)

  6. SENTIMENT BOTTOM SIGNALS
     - Short interest levels
     - Sell-side capitulation
     - Forum / community sentiment (microcap club, VIC, Substack discussions)

  7. REFLEXIVITY / FORCED-BUYING PATTERNS
     - Index inclusion candidate?
     - Forced sellers leaving the cap structure?
     - Activist escalation creating buying pressure?
