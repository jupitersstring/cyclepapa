WHAT MAKES A BUSINESS A MULTIBAGGER (FRAMEWORK)
================================================================================

Looking across the 21 names above, the patterns:

PATTERN 1: PERMANENT CAPITAL + COMPOUNDING ACQUISITIONS
  LOGC, HHH — vehicle structured to compound for decades via M&A,
  permitted by Berkshire-style permanent capital. Need: a great
  capital allocator at the helm.

PATTERN 2: FRANCHISE ECONOMICS + UNDERPRICED ROLLUP
  DRVN, APG, TVK.TO — franchise/distribution businesses with
  geographic density advantages. Need: discipline on acquisition prices.

PATTERN 3: SCALE/NETWORK EFFECTS + OPERATING LEVERAGE
  CVNA, MGNI, NEXN, HTHT — businesses with network effects where
  unit economics improve with scale. Need: continued share gains.

PATTERN 4: BINARY CLINICAL OR PERMITTING CATALYST
  ARTV, HYMC, ACMFF — single binary event drives 3-10x or -50%+.
  Need: specialist conviction on the binary outcome.

PATTERN 5: CYCLICAL UPSIDE + SUPPLY CONSTRAINT
  K92, ACMFF, LDO IM, NRP — commodity/cycle plays where producer
  margin expands faster than spot price. Need: cycle thesis to hold.

PATTERN 6: TURNAROUND/RESTRUCTURING WITH CONTROL CAPITAL
  PLCE, NEXN, CVNA — broken businesses with patient capital fixing.
  Need: operational execution + patient sponsor.

PATTERN 7: HIDDEN-ASSET / SUM-OF-PARTS
  WBD, HHH, ZEG.L, MAT (already in older list) — assets understated
  on balance sheet relative to public market value.
  Need: catalyst that forces revaluation.

PATTERN 8: ROYALTY/CONTRACT CASHFLOW + MULTIPLE EXPANSION
  NRP, TBPH, TPL (covered earlier) — irreplaceable contract streams
  trading at conservative multiples.
  Need: peer-multiple re-rate.

RISKS COMMON TO BUSINESS-FIRST INVESTING
================================================================================

1. EXECUTION RISK: Most multibaggers fail at operational execution, not
   thesis. Quarterly earnings + management quality matter more than
   smart-money tracking.

2. CAPITAL ALLOCATION: Multibaggers depend on disciplined deployment of
   incremental capital. ROIC > cost of capital required.

3. MARKET DYNAMICS: Consensus / momentum can crush valuation even if
   business fundamentals are intact. Be prepared for multi-year holds.

4. UNIT ECONOMICS DETERIORATION: Most "growth at any cost" stories fail
   when unit economics turn negative. Monitor closely.

5. INDUSTRY CYCLE: For cyclical names (K92, HYMC, ACMFF, NRP), the
   cycle thesis is more important than firm-specific execution.
