1. LOGC (ContextLogic Holdings) — $390M mcap
================================================================================

  mining of underground salt deposits in central New York via brine

  long-term contracts at $80-200/ton. 2025: $132M revenue, $55M adjusted
  EBITDA (41.9% margin), $11M net income (+123% YoY). Gross margin 37%
  and rising. Pricing power from oligopoly structure.

UNIT ECONOMICS:
  Cost per ton ~$50; price per ton ~$100-150. Salt is non-substitutable
  for most applications. Pricing rises 3-5%/yr; volume grows GDP+.

MOAT:
  - Unique cavern asset (45+ years remaining reserves)
  - Three-player oligopoly (Compass Minerals, Cargill, US Salt) — high
    transportation costs make regional dominance defensible
  - Customer switching costs (formulation testing, supply chain)
  - Replacement cost of brine caverns prohibitive

MARKET OPPORTUNITY:
  US industrial salt market $4B annually growing GDP+. Plus: this is the
  first acquisition of a permanent-capital vehicle structured to buy more
  cash-flow businesses. The vehicle preserves $2.9B of federal NOLs that
  shield future taxable income.

PATH TO 3-5x:
  Year 1-2: Realize US Salt pricing power; integrate.
  Year 2-5: Acquire 2-4 more cash-flow businesses at 8-12x EBITDA, using
            NOLs to shield income. Each $50M EBITDA acquisition adds 1x
            to base.
  Year 5: 4-5x EBITDA base = 4-5x equity at unchanged multiple.

WHAT BREAKS THE BUSINESS:
  - Section 382 IRS challenge to NOL utilization
  - Substitute deicing technology (calcium chloride, organic alternatives)
  - Salt demand contraction (climate trend toward less snow)

WHY NOW:
  US Salt acquisition closed Feb 2026. First full quarter as integrated
  holdco reports August 2026. Compounding hasn't started yet.
