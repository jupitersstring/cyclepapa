ILLIQUIDITY IS A FEATURE, NOT A BUG:

For long-duration patient capital, the fattest pitches often live in micro-cap
territory precisely because the marginal price-setter is gone. When a SITG
holder owns 30-60% of a microcap, there are essentially no sellers; the price
becomes a function of news catalysts + multi-year operational progress, not
short-term flows.

This brief includes 50 candidates across the liquidity spectrum, organized by
business pattern rather than market cap. Liquidity context noted for each but
not used to filter.

Pattern recap:
  1. Permanent capital + compounding M&A
  2. Franchise economics + underpriced rollup
  3. Scale/network effects + operating leverage
  4. Binary clinical or permitting catalyst
  5. Cyclical upside + supply constraint
  6. Turnaround/restructuring with control capital
  7. Hidden-asset / sum-of-parts
  8. Royalty/contract cashflow + multiple expansion
