  Variant perception gap (consensus PT vs smart-money target). 1
  Yartseva quantitative score >= 5/7 ........................ 1
  Margin of safety (FCF yield > 5% OR B/M > 0.5 OR cash floor) 1
  Near 12-month low (price range < 30% from bottom) .......... 1
  Risk-adjusted multiple potential (3:1 or better) ........... 1

MULTIBAGGER POTENTIAL (5 pts)
  Phelps/Mayer/Lynch pathway clear (compounder/turnaround/
    asset play / new product solving real problem) ........... 1
  TAM x penetration x execution capacity ..................... 1
  Time horizon credible (3-5x in 36mo OR 10x in 10yr) ........ 1
  Quality of business engine (moat + unit economics) ......... 1
  Owner-operator / patient-capital sponsor ................... 1
