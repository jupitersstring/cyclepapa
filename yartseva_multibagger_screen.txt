================================================================================
YARTSEVA'S SEVEN MULTIBAGGER CRITERIA (as commonly applied)
================================================================================

1. SMALL STARTING MARKET CAP
   Multibaggers almost always start small. Sweet spot: <$500M mcap.
   Below $100M is the highest-probability hunting ground. Above $2B is
   structural headwind to 10x.

2. HIGH INSIDER / CONCENTRATED OWNERSHIP (>20%)
   Founders + insiders + concentrated holders eat their own cooking and
   resist the redemption/quarterly-results cycle. Concentration creates
   long-duration capital.

3. HIGH AND STABLE/EXPANDING ROIC (>15-20%)
   Sustained high return on invested capital signals a real business
   moat. ROIC must be stable or expanding — not declining.

4. LONG REINVESTMENT RUNWAY
   The company must have profitable places to redeploy retained earnings
   at high returns. TAM penetration <10% ideal. Without runway, ROIC
   compounding stops.

5. PRICING POWER + MOAT
   Demonstrable pricing power (revenue growth > unit growth + inflation).
   Network effects, switching costs, regulatory moats, brand, or
   geographic density.

6. ASSET-LIGHT OR CAPITAL-EFFICIENT MODEL
   Either asset-light (SaaS, franchise, royalty) OR capital efficient
   per unit (high asset turnover). Capital-intensive businesses rarely
   multibag from operational compounding alone.

7. LOW LEVERAGE / NET CASH POSITION
   Multibaggers compound through cycles; over-leveraged businesses get
   wiped out in downturns. Net cash position or very low leverage.

PLUS implicit Y8: LIMITED ANALYST COVERAGE at time of investment.
Sub-3 analysts ideal. Coverage gap → mispricing → fat pitch.

PLUS Y9: BORING/UNCOOL INDUSTRY often features in multibaggers
(salt, royalty, franchise, niche industrials, owner-operator).

================================================================================
THE OPERATING TEMPLATE THAT PRODUCES MULTIBAGGERS (per Yartseva)
================================================================================

  REVENUE GROWTH RATE  × RETENTION RATIO  =  EARNINGS COMPOUND RATE

  Constellation Software: 30% growth × 80% retention = 24% compound
  Berkshire (early days): 15% × 100% retention = 15% compound
  TerraVest: 15% × 80% retention = 12% compound

  At 24% compound rate, stock 10x in ~11 years
  At 20% compound rate, stock 10x in ~13 years
  At 15% compound rate, stock 10x in ~16 years

The Tier 1 cohort's reinvestment rate × ROIC math works:
  VHI.V: 30% growth × 75% retention × 22% ROIC = ~5x compounder
  JYNT: 20% unit growth × 60% retention × 18% ROIC = ~3x compounder
  ZDC.V: 30% tower growth × 75% retention × 28% ROIC = ~6x compounder

That's why these are "Alchemy" multibaggers — operating economics that
produce the 10-bagger over the holding period, even without multiple
expansion.


================================================================================
KEY CAVEATS
================================================================================

1. THE YARTSEVA FRAMEWORK BIASES TOWARD COMPOUNDERS
   It explicitly excludes M&A endgame, post-restructuring recovery,
   cyclical commodity, and asset-realization plays. Those can be
   multibaggers via different paths (DRVN bid, HYMC restart, JOE
   land realization), but those paths are NOT Yartseva.

2. SCORING IS APPROXIMATION
   The 21-point scale uses commonly-cited thresholds rather than the
   exact paper's quantitative cuts. If you have the specific Yartseva
   thresholds (e.g., "ROIC > 18%" or "mcap < $300M") share and I'll
   refine.

3. SMART-MONEY POSITIONING IS RELEVANT BUT NOT DETERMINATIVE
   The companies above all happen to have smart-money concentration,
   but the framework filters on BUSINESS quality. SITG without
   compounding business = NOT a Yartseva multibagger.

4. PHASE 2-STAGE BIOTECH (ARTV) IS A SPECIAL CASE
   Pre-revenue means ROIC criterion fails by definition. But the
   structural setup (small mcap + concentrated ownership + platform +
   pricing power if approved + asset-light) fits the framework
   conceptually. Use binary catalyst framework as overlay.
