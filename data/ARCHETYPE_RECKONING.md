**Two main lessons:**

1. The archetypes did their job as **screens** — Yartseva, Spinoff and Hard Asset all surfaced winners (ROCK, KBR, NRP). But the empirical edge in those names is almost always carried by an **insider Form-4 cluster** that arrived after the original archetype tag — i.e., the archetype gives you a watch list, the insider cluster gives you the *signal*. Going forward, an archetype hit is a **candidate flag**, not an entry signal on its own.
2. **Activist 13D and Deep Drawdown — as standalone archetypes — should be retired** as Tier-1 entries. The base rate evidence is too negative. Names within them only qualify for Tier 1 when they *also* clear an empirical bucket (insider cluster, founder buy, or smart-money-underwater).
