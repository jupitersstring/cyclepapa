SCANNER METHODOLOGY REFERENCE
==============================

All measures implemented in ord_scanner.py, documented for reuse.
Each section states: what is computed, the formula, the thresholds,
and how it feeds the composite score.


================================================================================
1. ZIGZAG SWING DETECTION (Ord spec section 2.1)
================================================================================

Purpose: Identify swing highs and swing lows to define "legs" -- the
contiguous bar sequences between consecutive pivots.

Algorithm:
  - Walk forward through the bar series tracking the running extreme
    (highest high or lowest low since the last confirmed pivot).
  - A pivot is confirmed when price reverses by >= theta percent from
    the running extreme.
  - The final (unconfirmed) extreme is appended as a "tentative" pivot
    so the most recent in-progress leg can be evaluated.

Parameters:
  theta (% reversal required to confirm a swing):
    Weekly  = 5%   (SWING_THETA["W"] = 0.05)
    Monthly = 10%  (SWING_THETA["M"] = 0.10)
    Daily large-cap = 1.5-3% (not used in the scanner but available)
    Daily small-cap  = 3-5%

  Ord's "zoom = 0.25" in his charting software corresponds to surfacing
  sub-swings at 1/4 of the normal reversal threshold.

Output: List of pivots as (bar_index, price, direction) where
  direction = +1 for a high pivot, -1 for a low pivot.


================================================================================
2. LEG CONSTRUCTION AND ORD-VOLUME (Ord spec section 2.2, 3.1)
================================================================================

Purpose: Quantify the directional energy of each price swing.

Definition:
  A leg is the contiguous bar sequence between two adjacent pivots.
  Each leg has:
    - direction: "up" or "down"
    - start_idx, end_idx (inclusive bar indices)
    - start_price, end_price
    - n_bars = end_idx - start_idx + 1
    - Ord-Volume (OV) = arithmetic mean of volume over leg bars

Why arithmetic mean, not sum:
  Ord stresses that comparing total volume between legs of different
  durations is wrong. A 20-bar leg will have double the total volume of
  a 10-bar leg even at identical intensity. The mean normalises for
  duration, isolating participation intensity per unit time.

  Formula: OV = sum(volume[start..end]) / n_bars

  Sum-of-volume comparisons are explicitly forbidden by Ord.


================================================================================
3. ORD-VOLUME BUY SIGNAL (Ord spec section 3.2)
================================================================================

Purpose: Detect a bullish reversal setup where selling energy (volume)
has dried up on the decline, and buying energy is expanding on the
subsequent rally.

Pattern (looking at the last 4 legs):
  legs[-4] = prev_down (establishes the "prior swing low" at its end)
  legs[-3] = prev_up
  legs[-2] = L_down (the decline being tested)
  legs[-1] = L_current (the rally in progress)

Three conditions must ALL be met (spec section 3.2):

  (a) Volume shrinkage (OR comparator -- either leg satisfies):
      OV(L_down) <= 0.50 * OV(prev_up)
      OR
      OV(L_down) <= 0.50 * OV(prev_down)

  (b) Probe: L_down's swing low is below the prior swing low
      (i.e. L_down.end_price < prev_down.end_price)
      This means price broke below the prior support level.

  (c) Close-back: current price has closed back above the prior swing low
      (i.e. close[now] > prev_down.end_price)
      This means the breakdown was rejected -- a "shakeout".

Confirmation (within <= 5 bars after trigger):
  OV(L_current) >= 1.50 * OV(L_down)
  i.e. the rally leg's average volume is at least 50% higher than the
  decline leg's average volume.

Strength scoring based on shrinkage ratio:
  <= 0.25  (>= 75% shrinkage)  = STRONG
  0.25-0.50                    = GOOD
  0.50-0.55                    = MARGINAL (Ord accepts down to ~46%)
  > 0.55                       = NO SIGNAL

Expansion scoring (confirmation quality):
  >= 3.00 (>= 200% expansion)  = EXPLOSIVE
  1.50-3.00                    = CONFIRMED
  1.30-1.50                    = WEAK (the scanner's "loose" threshold)
  1.00-1.30                    = RANGE-BOUND expected
  < 1.00                       = FAILED -- exit

Scanner implementation:
  - "signal" (strict): all three conditions + strength is not None
  - "signal_loose": drops the probe requirement, uses 1.30x for confirm
  - Both are reported so partial setups surface in the ranking

Index adjustment (spec section 3.4, NOT used in stock scanner):
  Indices don't exhibit 50% leg shrinkage. For index application:
    Trigger shrinkage:  <= 0.70 (30% shrinkage, not 50%)
    Confirmation:       >= 1.10 (10% expansion, not 50%)


================================================================================
4. SHAKEOUT RETEST (Ord spec section 4.2(f) -- False Breakout Bottom)
================================================================================

Purpose: Detect a Wyckoff-style shakeout where price breaks below a
prior swing low on shrinking volume, then reverses. This is a bar-level
volume comparator (vs Ord-Volume's leg-level comparator).

Algorithm:
  - Identify the prior swing low: the end of legs[-4] (prev_down leg).
  - The "origin bar" is the bar at that swing low's index.
  - Search within the most recent down leg (legs[-2]) for any bar whose
    Low <= prior swing low price (a probe).
  - Compare the minimum volume among probe bars to the origin bar volume.

Thresholds (spec section 4.1):
  probe_vol / origin_vol <= 0.92  = reversal signal (energy insufficient)
  0.92 - 0.97                    = "no man's land" -- no signal
  >= 0.97                        = continuation expected (energy sufficient)

Additional condition:
  Current close must be back above the prior swing low (close-back).

Signal: TRUE if ratio <= 0.92 AND close-back is true.

The tighter the shrinkage (20%, 50%, 75% less volume on probe), the
higher the conviction. A probe at 50% of origin volume is very strong.

Critical implementation note (Ord p. 69):
  The highest-volume bar and the lowest-price bar at a climax are
  frequently NOT the same bar (often offset by 1 day). Use the
  highest-volume bar for volume comparisons and the lowest-price bar
  for price comparisons. Treating them as the same bar destroys the
  signal in ~30% of cases.

First-High/First-Low anchoring rule (spec section 4.2(c)):
  When multiple retests occur (3rd, 4th, etc.), always compare volume
  to the FIRST peak/trough in the cluster, never the most recent one.
  Sequential mild declines can mask cumulative weakness if you re-anchor.


================================================================================
5. BOLLINGER / KELTNER SQUEEZE (TTM-style)
================================================================================

Purpose: Detect volatility compression (a "loaded spring") that precedes
large directional moves. When Bollinger Bands contract inside Keltner
Channels, price is coiling. The release (BB expanding back outside KC)
often produces the breakout.

Computation:
  Bollinger Bands:
    ma     = SMA(close, 20)
    sd     = StdDev(close, 20)
    bb_up  = ma + 2.0 * sd
    bb_dn  = ma - 2.0 * sd

  Keltner Channels:
    tr     = max(high-low, |high-prev_close|, |low-prev_close|)
    atr    = SMA(tr, 20)
    kc_up  = ma + 1.5 * atr
    kc_dn  = ma - 1.5 * atr

  Squeeze state:
    in_squeeze = (bb_up < kc_up) AND (bb_dn > kc_dn)
    i.e. Bollinger Bands are INSIDE Keltner Channels on both sides.

  Squeeze release:
    released = squeeze was active in any of the prior 7 bars AND
               squeeze is OFF on the current bar.

  Bandwidth percentile:
    bw = (bb_up - bb_dn) / ma
    bw_pctile = rank of current bw within the full history (0 = tightest
    ever, 1 = widest ever).

Scoring:
  released = TRUE                      -> +3 to ord_score
  in_squeeze AND bw_pctile <= 0.25     -> +2 (loaded but not yet fired)
  bw_pctile <= 0.25 (even without KC)  -> +1 (compressed)

Parameters:
  BB_LENGTH = 20
  BB_MULT   = 2.0
  KC_LENGTH = 20
  KC_MULT   = 1.5
  SQUEEZE_BW_PCTL = 0.25  (bottom quartile = compressed)


================================================================================
6. VOLATILITY ASYMMETRY (up-bar vs down-bar energy)
================================================================================

Purpose: Measure whether buying activity (volume + range on up bars)
exceeds selling activity (volume + range on down bars). Persistent
positive asymmetry indicates accumulation; negative indicates distribution.

Computation (over the last 20 bars):
  Classify each bar: up if close > prior close, down if close < prior close.

  up_vol   = mean volume of up bars
  dn_vol   = mean volume of down bars
  up_range = mean (high - low) of up bars
  dn_range = mean (high - low) of down bars

  vol_ratio   = up_vol / dn_vol     (capped at 5.0)
  range_ratio = up_range / dn_range (capped at 5.0)

  asymmetry = (vol_ratio + range_ratio) / 2.0

Interpretation:
  >= 1.50  = strong bullish asymmetry, up bars dominate  -> +2 to score
  >= 1.20  = moderate bullish asymmetry                  -> +1 to score
  ~1.00    = neutral (balanced buying/selling)
  < 0.80   = bearish asymmetry (not scored in long-only scanner)

Parameters:
  ASYMMETRY_WINDOW = 20 bars
  ASYM_MIN_BULLISH = 1.20


================================================================================
7. QULLAMAGGIE BREAKOUT SETUP
================================================================================

Purpose: Detect Kristjan Qullamaggie's tight-range-breakout pattern.
The setup is: a relative strength leader consolidates in a tight range
near highs with volume drying up, then breaks out on expanding volume.

Components (each scored independently, summed to q_score 0-10):

  (a) Moving average stack and trend:
      MA10 > MA20 > MA50 with close > MA10 AND MA10/MA20 both rising
      Full stack + rising = +2
      Stack only          = +1

  (b) Relative strength (prior multi-bar returns):
      ret_13 = 13-bar percentage return
      ret_26 = 26-bar percentage return
      ret_26 >= 25% AND ret_13 > 0  = +2
      ret_26 >= 15%                 = +1

  (c) Range compression (tight consolidation):
      compression = ATR(5) / ATR(20)
      Measures recent range vs longer-term range.
      <= 0.70  = very tight  -> +2
      <= 0.85  = tight       -> +1

  (d) Volume dry-up in consolidation:
      volratio = avg_volume(5) / avg_volume(20)
      <= 0.75           -> +1
      0.75-0.90         -> +1

  (e) Breakout proximity (how close to breaking out):
      proximity = close / max(high, 20 bars)
      >= 0.97 (within 3% of 20-bar high)  -> +2
      >= 0.93 (within 7%)                 -> +1

  (f) Active breakout bar:
      close > max(high, prior 20 bars excluding current)
      AND current volume > 1.3 * avg(volume, prior 20 bars)
      -> +1

Total q_score range: 0-10 (sum of all components).


================================================================================
8. CONSOLIDATION LENGTH / ORD'S "CAUSE" (Ord spec section 9.2)
================================================================================

Purpose: Measure how long price has been range-bound. Ord asserts that
longer consolidation ("cause") leads to larger subsequent moves ("effect").
In Wyckoff terms, the sideways range is accumulating energy; the longer
the cause, the more energy stored.

Algorithm:
  Walk backward from the current bar. At each step, expand the envelope
  to include that bar's high and low:
    h = max(h, bar.high)
    l = min(l, bar.low)
  If (h - l) / mid > tolerance, stop. The count of bars included before
  stopping is the consolidation length.

  This measures how many recent bars fit inside a single price envelope
  of the specified width. A stock that has been range-bound for 12 weeks
  within a 12% band returns cause_bars = 12.

Parameters:
  tolerance:
    Weekly  = 0.12 (12% envelope)
    Monthly = 0.20 (20% envelope)
  cap = 104 bars maximum lookback

Scoring:
  cause_bars >= 12  -> +2 to ord_score
  cause_bars >= 6   -> +1 to ord_score

Interpretation:
  cause_bars = 3-5:  short pause, minor move expected
  cause_bars = 6-12: meaningful consolidation, decent move potential
  cause_bars = 13+:  extended cause, large move expected on breakout
  cause_bars = 20+:  major base, watch for trend-change breakout


================================================================================
9. REGIME-CONDITIONAL RELATIVE STRENGTH (SPY-based)
================================================================================

Purpose: Determine whether a stock outperforms SPY across all market
conditions -- not just in bull markets (which is trivially achieved by
high-beta names). A true "all-weather" stock generates excess returns
when the market is rising, falling, AND going sideways.

Step 1 -- Classify market regime:
  Download SPY for the same timeframe and period as the stock universe.
  Compute SPY's rolling lookback-bar return:
    weekly:  13-bar (quarter) rolling return
    monthly: 6-bar (half-year) rolling return

  Split the distribution of this rolling return into terciles:
    Bottom third = "down" regime (SPY falling)
    Middle third = "chop" regime (SPY sideways/mixed)
    Top third    = "up" regime (SPY rising)

  Each bar in the dataset gets a regime label. This gives roughly equal
  sample sizes across all three regimes (~83 bars each for 5yr weekly,
  ~131 each for 33yr monthly).

Step 2 -- Compute per-regime excess return:
  For each stock, align its returns with the SPY regime frame on dates.

  For each regime r in {up, chop, down}:
    excess[r] = mean(stock_daily_return - spy_daily_return)
                over all bars where regime == r

  The result is in per-bar units. Multiply by 100 for display as
  percentage per bar.

  rs_up   = mean excess return per bar in up-regime bars
  rs_chop = mean excess return per bar in chop-regime bars
  rs_down = mean excess return per bar in down-regime bars

  Positive rs_down means the stock falls less than SPY (or rises) when
  the broad market is declining. This is the hardest alpha to generate
  and the most predictive of future resilience.

Step 3 -- All-weather flag:
  all_weather = TRUE iff rs_up > 0 AND rs_chop > 0 AND rs_down > 0

  This means the stock has historically beaten SPY in every market
  condition over the sample window. Names like high-beta tech will
  show strong rs_up but negative rs_down (they crash harder in selloffs).
  True all-weather names outperform regardless.

Scoring:
  all_weather = TRUE  -> +3 to combined score

Parameters:
  REGIME_LOOKBACK["W"] = 13 (bars for SPY rolling return, weekly)
  REGIME_LOOKBACK["M"] = 6  (bars for SPY rolling return, monthly)


================================================================================
10. TREND REGIME FILTER (simple)
================================================================================

Purpose: Confirm the stock is in an uptrend on the active timeframe.

Computation:
  ma50 = SMA(close, 50)
  above_ma50 = close > ma50

Scoring:
  above_ma50 = TRUE  -> +1 to ord_score


================================================================================
11. COMPOSITE SCORING AND RANKING
================================================================================

The scanner computes two sub-scores and one combined score:

  ord_score = sum of:
    Ord-Volume signal strength:
      strict signal:  STRONG +4, GOOD +3, MARGINAL +2
        + confirmed (expand >= 1.50)       +2
        + confirmed_loose (expand >= 1.30) +1
      loose signal (no probe): STRONG +3, GOOD +2, MARGINAL +1
        + confirmed_loose                  +1
    Shakeout retest signal TRUE:           +3
    Squeeze released:                      +3
    In squeeze + bw_pctile <= 0.25:        +2
    bw_pctile <= 0.25 (no squeeze):        +1
    Asymmetry >= 1.50:                     +2
    Asymmetry >= 1.20:                     +1
    Above MA50:                            +1
    Cause bars >= 12:                      +2
    Cause bars >= 6:                       +1

  q_score (0-10) = sum of Qullamaggie components (see section 7)

  combined = ord_score + q_score + (3 if all_weather)

Ranking: sort by combined DESC, then all_weather DESC, then ord_score DESC,
then q_score DESC, then asymmetry DESC.


================================================================================
12. OUTPUT COLUMNS REFERENCE
================================================================================

  ticker       : stock symbol
  combined     : composite score (ord_score + q_score + all_weather bonus)
  ord_score    : Ord / squeeze / asymmetry / cause sub-score
  q_score      : Qullamaggie sub-score (0-10)
  close        : last closing price

  ov_sig       : Ord-Volume strict buy signal (True/False)
  ov_sig_loose : Ord-Volume loose buy signal (probe dropped)
  ov_str       : signal strength (STRONG / GOOD / MARGINAL / NONE)
  ov_shrink    : OV(L_down) / OV(comparator) -- lower = more bullish
  ov_expand    : OV(L_current) / OV(L_down) -- higher = more confirmed
  ov_probed    : did L_down break below prior swing low?
  ov_closed_back : has price closed back above prior swing low?

  spv_sig      : shakeout (false breakout bottom) signal (True/False)
  spv_ratio    : probe_vol / origin_vol -- lower = stronger shakeout

  squeeze      : currently in BB-inside-KC squeeze (True/False)
  released     : squeeze just released in last 7 bars (True/False)
  bw_pctile    : BB width percentile (0 = tightest ever, 1 = widest)

  asym         : up/down volatility asymmetry (>1 = bullish, <1 = bearish)

  above_ma50   : close > 50-period MA (True/False)

  q_stacked    : MA10 > MA20 > MA50 with close > MA10 (True/False)
  q_ret13      : 13-bar return (decimal, e.g. 0.15 = +15%)
  q_ret26      : 26-bar return
  q_compress   : ATR(5) / ATR(20) range compression (lower = tighter)
  q_volratio   : vol(5) / vol(20) volume dry-up (lower = drier)
  q_prox       : close / 20-bar high (1.0 = at high, 0.90 = 10% below)
  q_brk        : active breakout bar (close > prior 20-bar high on volume)

  cause_bars   : consolidation length in bars (Ord's "cause")

  rs_up        : mean excess return per bar vs SPY in up-regime (pct)
  rs_chop      : mean excess return per bar vs SPY in chop-regime (pct)
  rs_down      : mean excess return per bar vs SPY in down-regime (pct)
  all_weather  : positive excess in all three regimes (True/False)

14. USAGE
================================================================================

  # Full SP500 scan, weekly + monthly, all scores
  python ord_scanner.py --tf both --top 30 --min-score 0

  # Weekly only, all-weather filter
  python ord_scanner.py --tf W --aw-only --top 50

  # Monthly only, minimum combined score of 8
  python ord_scanner.py --tf M --min-score 8 --top 30

  # Custom ticker list
  python ord_scanner.py --tickers AAPL MSFT NVDA AMZN --tf W

  # Output to specific directory
  python ord_scanner.py --out-dir ./results

  The scanner writes full rankings (all tickers, all scores) to CSV
  regardless of display filters. CSVs go to --out-dir as:
    ord_scan_weekly.csv
    ord_scan_monthly.csv

  The GitHub Actions workflow runs automatically on push to the feature
  branch and on a weekly Sunday-23:00-UTC cron. Results are committed
  back to the branch under results/ and also uploaded as artifacts.
