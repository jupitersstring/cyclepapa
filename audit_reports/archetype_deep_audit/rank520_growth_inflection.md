# Rank 5–20 deep audit — GROWTH & INFLECTION archetypes

Scope: names ranked 5–20 by `entry_today_asymmetry` in each archetype (16 per sheet). Verdicts verified against `archetype_tags.py` rule legs. Base-effect is treated as ACCEPTABLE for growth archetypes per the owner; it is flagged only when the same name is a one-off M&A/licensing spike or a data artifact, or is used in a rule that claims durability/inflection. `$` = USD millions. `REV~` = mcap/p_s proxy. Worst-first within each block.

Cross-cutting mechanics found (detail in per-archetype notes):
- **A. `is_operating` is porous.** It is `~(sector contains financ|real estate|utilit)`, so a NULL `sector` bypasses it; and several rules omit the gate entirely. Banks, insurers, REITs, utilities leak.
- **B. Backward-looking `*_lindy` / `n_yrs_*_pos` gates with no current floor.** A trailing multi-year ROIC window keeps qualifying a business that has since started melting or turned loss-making.
- **C. No revenue floor** on most growth/microcap rules → sub-$20M (even sub-$5M) revenue nanos where % is noise. `sustainable_scaler` already carries `_srev>=20e6`; nothing else does.
- **D. Cross-country EV / net-cash artifacts** (`net_cash_pct_mcap`>1, near-zero or negative `ev_ebitda`/`ev_sales`) laundered into "cheap"/"quality"; duplicate ADR lines double-count.
- **E. Margin/cost inflection legs fire while revenue DECLINES.** `oper_lev_any` / `margin_shock_any` / `fcf_margin>0` read cost-cut blips as inflection. The `rev_yoy>0` (G8) guard exists in `fixed_cost_demand_shock` & `regime_cyclical` but is missing from the ROIC/levered/liger/micro-activist inflection rules.

## GROWTH

### arch_bab_becoming — WRONG ≈ 11/16 (worst growth sheet)
Thesis: a business *de-risking* toward the boring-safe low-beta profile (margins expanding, cash inflecting, deleveraging). Instead the sheet is dominated by loss-makers and collapsing shells. **RFT.AX** Rectifier Tech: rev −38%, EBITDA margin **−49%**, ROCE −24%, mom −50% — the antithesis of de-risking. **BAI.SI** Digilife: **$0.3M revenue** shell, nde −35. **JFU** 9F: EV/EBITDA **−896**, nde −96 (net-cash artifact), rev −6.5%. **TTEC**: ROCE −9.7%, nde −93.7, rev −3.2%, mom −60%. **SPLIL.NS** rev **−65%**; **047820.KQ** Chorokbaem rev −30%; **4170.T** rev −3.7%. **DDEJF** Dundee is a holdco (financial) mislabeled Consumer Staples; **BVNRY** is a drug developer. **RULE ERROR:** the de-risking legs are too weak — `((ebitda_margin_delta>=0.01)|interval_inflect_any) & ((fcf_inflection>0)|(ebitda_inflection>0)|(fcf_margin_v>0.0))` lets a single asset-sale-driven positive FCF on a money-losing, shrinking name pass; no `is_operating`, no positive-EBITDA floor, no revenue-direction/scale guard. **FIX:** require `ebitda_margin>0` (use `ebitda_margin_sane`), `is_operating`, a revenue floor (`revenue_ttm>=20e6`), and `rev_yoy>−0.05 | rev_accel>0`; drop the standalone `fcf_margin_v>0` OR-leg.

### arch_midcap_garp — WRONG ≈ 9/16
Quality/GARP at ≥$2B. Two failure modes. (1) **EV/EBITDA artifacts**: Nitori (**NCLTF & NCLTY — same company listed twice**) EV/EBITDA 0.034/0.043 with net cash 230%/220% of mcap; Ericsson 0.381; Galaxy (**GXYEF & GXYYY — duplicate ADRs**) 0.106/0.042; Aker 0.151. `_val_good` uses `1/ev_ebitda` as an EBITDA yield with no sanity floor, so a near-zero/negative artifact EV passes `_val_good` spuriously. (2) **Declining revenue passing the "growing" leg** via `_ebit_g>0` alone: Nitori rev −1.8%, Ericsson −8.2%, Raven (RV1.F, likely stale — CNH acquired Raven in 2021) with **ROCE −0.173**. **RULE ERROR:** no EV/EBITDA sanity clamp, no `net_cash_pct_sane`, no `is_operating`/positive-ROCE gate, no ADR dedupe; `_ey_good_growing` accepts EBIT growth while revenue falls. **FIX:** clamp `ev_ebitda>=2` (or use a sane band) before inverting to a yield, require `net_cash_pct_sane`, require `roce>0`, and require `rev_yoy>=0` alongside `_ebit_g>0`.

### arch_cheap_per_roiic — WRONG ≈ 6/16
"Pay little per unit of reinvestment yield" — a durable-compounder claim. But it gates on the trailing `roiic_lindy>0.10` / `cheap_per_roiic_lindy` with **no current-growth or not-declining guard**, so melting ice cubes qualify: **CATO** rev 3y CAGR −4.9%, EBITDA margin **0.4%**, EV/EBITDA 134; **THRY** rev −6.4% YoY / −13.2% 3y, mom −73%, net *debt*; **RMNI** ROCE **−0.736**, P/E 72; **SBC** rev −15.5%; **CRCT** rev −0.5%/−7.2% 3y; **TDC** rev −3.5%. **HGBL** is Financials (no `is_operating` gate here). **RULE ERROR:** mechanic B — historical ROIIC with no live check. **FIX:** add `is_operating`, and require `(rev_yoy>0)|(rev_3y_cagr>0)` plus `roic_after_sbc>0` (or `roiic_acceleration_v>=0`) so a shrinking business fails.

### arch_wolf_trifecta — WRONG ≈ 5/16
Wolf = double-digit grower + operating leverage + improving margin bought cheap; his siblings (`wolf_turnaround`/`value_catalyst`) carry `& is_operating`, **this one does not**. Leaks: **TETAA** Teton Advisors — **Financials**, **$2.5M revenue**, ROCE **−0.028**, P/S 9.8 but EV/sales 0.10 (net-cash artifact making it look cheap); **6912.KL** Pasdec — **Real Estate**, EBITDA margin **0.818** (one-off asset-sale margin, above the sane 0.6 band), rev +232%; **1281.HK** LongiTech — **Utilities**; **BENGALT.BO** **$5.5M revenue** nano; **2348.HK** Dawnrays is a drug developer. **RULE ERROR:** missing `is_operating`, missing `ebitda_margin_sane`, no revenue floor. **FIX:** append `& is_operating & (ebitda_margin_sane) & (revenue_ttm>=20e6)`.

### arch_bab_low_beta — WRONG ≈ 6/16
The paper's long side is safe/profitable/genuinely-low-beta quality; the quality gate is meant to screen out illiquid nanos whose *measured* low beta is a non-trading artifact. But `adv = s('avg_dollar_volume', 1e12)` — **missing ADV defaults to 1e12 and auto-passes the `adv>=1e5` liquidity floor**, so foreign nanos with no ADV data sail through: **BENGALT.BO** $13M/REV~5.5, **SHRIDINE.BO** $22M/REV~7.3, **TPP.BK** $15M/REV~9.1, **031510.KQ** $19M. Plus declining "quality": **2033.HK** rev −10%/ROCE 4%, **014570.KQ** Korean Drug rev −14%; **6912.KL** Pasdec (Real Estate, 82% one-off margin); **2348.HK** drug developer. **RULE ERROR:** the ADV default (`1e12`) inverts the liquidity guard for missing data; no `is_operating`; no revenue floor. **FIX:** default missing ADV to `0` (or require ADV present) so illiquid unknowns fail; add `is_operating` and a revenue floor.

### arch_bab_multibagger — WRONG ≈ 5/16
Same population/mechanics as `bab_low_beta` (identical ADV-default and no-`is_operating`/no-rev-floor issues): illiquid nanos **BENGALT.BO/SHRIDINE.BO/TPP.BK/031510.KQ**, declining names **2033.HK** (−10%), **014570.KQ** (−14%) passing via `bab_cheap_leg` (fcf_yield≥0.08) rather than any multibagger inflection, Real-Estate **6912.KL**, drug developer **2348.HK**. Same FIX as above.

### arch_exceptional_evsg — WRONG ≈ 5/16
EVSG = cheap EV/sales *relative to growth*. When growth is a one-off explosion the denominator inflates and EVSG looks "exceptional" — a PSG/EVSG artifact. **B9A.F BioArctic** is the clean example: **drug developer**, rev **+345%** (a lecanemab licensing/royalty step, lumpy not durable), P/E 308 — a one-off licensing spike dressed as exceptional growth-per-multiple (flag per the one-off M&A/licensing rule). Also **ZENIFIB.BO** REV~4.8 and **BENGALT.BO** REV~5.5 (sub-$20M base, % meaningless); **TUGU.JK** is an insurer (sector NULL → bypasses `is_operating`) with EV/EBITDA −2.0. **RULE ERROR:** no revenue floor; NULL-sector `is_operating` bypass (mechanic A); no lumpy-revenue guard for one-off royalty/milestone names. **FIX:** revenue floor ≥$20M; treat NULL sector as non-operating (or exclude `is_drug_developer` where revenue is milestone-lumpy); cap the growth input used in EVSG.

### arch_cheap_sales_scaler — WRONG ≈ 4/16
Cheap-on-sales grower inflecting to profit. Leaks sub-$20M nanos where the "scaling" is noise: **ZENIFIB.BO** $2.2M mc / REV~4.8 (FCF negative), **NPK.BK** $3.9M / REV~6.8; and drug developer **2348.HK** Dawnrays. Otherwise on-thesis. **RULE ERROR:** the `p_s>=0.10` floor guards mcap artifacts, not tiny-revenue bases; no revenue floor. **FIX:** add `revenue_ttm>=20e6` (and optionally an mcap floor) — port the `sustainable_scaler` guard.

### arch_tenbagger_path — WRONG ≈ 3/16
Rule has `mcap>0` and `ps>0 & <30` but **no revenue floor**, so 10x arithmetic runs off tiny bases: **BENGALT.BO** REV~5.5, **Linkgenesis** REV~12.9, plus the `_g_confirmed` "≥2 bases agree" collapses to essentially one base for non-US names (rev_3y_cagr/5y are NULL for nearly the whole sheet, leaving rev_yoy + rev_qoq_ttm). **RULE ERROR:** no revenue floor; multi-base confirmation degenerates when EDGAR CAGRs are absent. **FIX:** `revenue_ttm>=20e6`; require `_g_confirmed` to draw on ≥2 *distinct* bases or fall back to a stricter single-base threshold.

### arch_tenbagger_credible — WRONG ≈ 2/16
Inherits `tenbagger_path` then adds owner-cash + stable-share reality gates (works well). Residual: **B9A.F BioArctic** — a **drug developer** whose +345% revenue is a lecanemab licensing/royalty step (lumpy milestone income, not the durable owner-cash compounding "credible" claims), P/E 308 making the implied return suspect; and **BENGALT.BO** REV~5.5 (sub-$20M base inherited from `path`). **FIX:** revenue floor on the parent; add a lumpy-revenue/`is_drug_developer` exclusion so milestone royalties don't read as durable compounding.

### arch_growth_algo — WRONG ≈ 2/16 (mostly clean)
Flywheel: growth + operating leverage + FCF compounding, cheap on EV/FCF. Base-effect names (**1815.HK** CSMall +209%, **088130.KQ** Dong A Eltek +234%) are acceptable per owner. Real misfits are sub-$20M bases: **AKP.BK** $9M/REV~13, and 1433.HK/6063.T are borderline. **RULE ERROR:** no revenue floor. **FIX:** `revenue_ttm>=20e6`.

### arch_sustainable_scaler — WRONG ≈ 2/16 (cleanest sheet)
By design carries `is_operating & mcap<2e9 & revenue_ttm>=20e6 & shares flat/down & durable growth & self-funding & not-pricey`. Residual leaks are the NULL-sector `is_operating` bypass: **TUGU.JK** (insurer, sector NULL, EV/EBITDA −2.0) and drug developer **002758.SZ** ZJAMP (EBITDA margin 2.7%); **TCID.JK** self-funds despite negative CFO (fcf>cfo asset-sale). **FIX:** treat NULL sector as non-operating; optionally exclude `is_drug_developer`.

## INFLECTION

### arch_roic_inflect — WRONG ≈ 11/16 (worst inflection sheet)
"Latest ROIC crossed zero from below, confirmed by cash-ROIC." Gate: `((roic_inflect|cash_roic_inflect)==1) & (cash_roic_lindy>0)` — **no `is_operating`, no current-ROIC-positive floor.** Result: (1) **four financials** — FXNC (bank), NTRSO (Northern Trust), SRBK (bank), COHN (Cohen & Co) — where ROIC/EBITDA are meaningless (EBITDA margin shows 0.0). (2) Names with **currently negative** `roic_after_sbc`: BANL −0.195 (& rev −9%, EBITDA neg), TUYA −0.079, QTWO −0.123, SSTK −0.016 — passing on the binary flag + a trailing `cash_roic_lindy>0`. (3) Melting: THRY (rev −6.4%/−13.2% 3y, mom −73%), VTSI (rev −15%), SIRI (rev −1.4%/−1.7%). **RULE ERROR:** mechanics A + B — historical lindy confirmation, no operating gate, no live-ROIC floor. **FIX:** add `is_operating`, require `roic_after_sbc>0` (or `roce>0`) *today*, and `rev_yoy>−0.05 | rev_accel>0`.

### arch_liger_lagging_inflect — WRONG ≈ 9/16

Instead a majority are **declining or flat** revenue passing via the `oper_lev_any` (margin/cost) leg: **ILINK.BK** rev **−38%**, EBITDA margin −4%, ROCE −18.5%, nde 13; **900926.SS** Baosight −17%; **089600.KQ** Nasmedia −15%; **QES.SI** −7% (net cash 362% artifact); **AKW.PA** −5%; **NRG.PA** −3%; **5843.KL** −5%; **043610.KQ** Genie Music flat with **ROCE −0.224**. ### arch_levered_inflection — WRONG ≈ 7/16

### arch_micro_activist_inflect — WRONG ≈ 6/16
Microcap + short-term inflection + cheap + clean balance sheet. `inflection_now` accepts `ebitda_margin_delta>=0.02 | rev_accel>=0.05` and there is **no `is_operating` gate**, so a data-artifact collapse passes: **PRIMEPRO.BO** rev **−100%** (revenue to zero), **Real Estate**; **JAMESWARREN.BO** EV/EBITDA −0.42 & EV/sales −0.32 (negative-EV artifacts), rev −2.2%, one-off 75% margin; **SIAM.BK** rev **−29%**; **IRC.BK** rev −2.1%. ### arch_double_inflect — WRONG ≈ 5/16
"Both NOPAT-ROIC and cash-ROIC crossed zero from below." Fires on **noise-level** crossings and **restructuring** inflections with nothing on magnitude or revenue direction: **BODI** Beachbody rev **−40%** YoY / −29% 3y — ROIC crossed positive on cost cuts as revenue craters (a restructuring artifact, not a reinvestment inflection); **HOLO** MicroCloud ROCE 0.003 / ROIC 0.001 with EV/EBITDA −568, nde −627 (pure noise + artifact); **MVST** Microvast ROIC 1.4%, nde 75, mom −87% (distress); **IDN** roic_after_sbc **−0.057**. **SSRM & SSRGF** are the same company (Ssr Mining) listed twice. **RULE ERROR:** gates only the two binary flags — no magnitude floor, no revenue-not-collapsing guard, no ADR dedupe. **FIX:** require `cash_roic_lindy>=0.05` (or current `roic_after_sbc>=0.05`) and `rev_yoy>−0.10 | rev_accel>0`; dedupe ADR lines.

## Top-5 highest-impact fixes (worst-first, by names corrected across sheets)

1. **Add a current-state floor to every backward-looking-ROIC rule** (`roic_inflect`, `double_inflect`, `cheap_per_roiic`, `capital_light_pivot`): require `roic_after_sbc>0` (or `roce>0`) *today* and `rev_yoy>−0.05 | rev_accel>0`, not just a trailing `*_lindy`/`n_yrs_*_pos`. Fixes ~24 names (CATO, THRY, RMNI, BANL, TUYA, QTWO, SSTK, BODI, HURC, …) — the single biggest melting-ice-cube/noise leak.
2. **Close the `is_operating` hole**: (a) treat NULL `sector` as non-operating, and (b) add `& is_operating` to the rules that omit it — `roic_inflect`, `double_inflect`, `levered_inflection`, `micro_activist_inflect`, `wolf_trifecta`, `cheap_per_roiic`, `bab_*`. Removes banks/insurers/REITs/utilities (FXNC, NTRSO, SRBK, COHN, LX, TUGU, PRIMEPRO, SAMCO, TETAA, Pasdec, CWCO, LongiTech).
3. **Port `sustainable_scaler`'s `revenue_ttm>=20e6` floor to all growth/microcap rules** (`tenbagger_path`, `cheap_sales_scaler`, `exceptional_evsg`, `growth_algo`, `wolf_trifecta`/`turnaround`/`value_catalyst`, `bab_*`, `micro_activist_inflect`, `fixed_cost_demand_shock`, `regime_cyclical`). Kills sub-$20M nanos where % is meaningless (Bengal Tea, ZENIFIB, NPK, DeTai, BAI.SI, WHLM, Kawase).
4. **Require a revenue-direction floor on the margin/cost inflection legs** so `oper_lev_any`/`margin_shock_any`/`fcf_margin>0` cannot fire on a shrinking business — add `(rev_yoy>0)|(rev_accel>0)` (the existing G8 guard) to `liger_lagging_inflect`, `levered_inflection`, `roic_inflect`, `double_inflect`, `bab_becoming`, `micro_activist_inflect`. Fixes the declining-revenue "inflection" epidemic (ILINK −38%, Neungyule −33%, SIAM −29%, Baosight −17%, RFT −38%, etc.).
5. **Sanity-clamp EV/net-cash artifacts and dedupe ADRs**: reject `ev_ebitda`/`ev_sales`≤0 or near-zero before using them as cheapness, gate on `net_cash_pct_sane` (≤1.0), and collapse duplicate cross-listings. Fixes `midcap_garp` (Nitori×2, Galaxy×2, Ericsson, Aker), `bab_becoming` (JFU −896), `regime_cyclical` (Sewon 364%, Polimex −0.04), `double_inflect` (SSRM/SSRGF), `exceptional_evsg`/`micro_activist` (TUGU, JAMESWARREN). Also default missing ADV to 0 (not 1e12) in the BAB liquidity floor so illiquid-beta artifacts stop auto-passing.
